# Node of Arcane Displacement
 
 Highly refined rock that when shattered, rips a small hole in the weave and sucks a spell within 60ft through it. Basic counterspell rules, 3rd or below auto-succeed, higher must role, using intelligence modifier, using intelligence modifier. Break as a reaction. Cannot be broken without the intent to be broken. 
 
 It punctures a hole through the [[Planes of existence]] making a small but permanent hole to the [[Plane of Negative Energy]].
