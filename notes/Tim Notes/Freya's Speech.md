# Freya's Speech

*What we want to convey:*
 * *Dumb, maybe crazy*
 * *Hard to control*
 * *Probably harmless if you're not a Gruung or Lectern*

Wayfinders!

*Deploy claws and roar, break something nearby, preferably the lectern, keep a piece of what you break and use it to point to people while you give your speech*

My name is the Grungslayer, AKA Freya Polaris.

Honestly, I'm not sure why they invited me to speak. Rawley tells me that I am the youngest Wayfinder to transnavigamate the continent. That's what they wanted me to talk about, and that's cool. But what is more cool is the time that my buddies and I killed enough Gruung to make a cloak out of their skin. Why? Because they KILLED EROS and tried to destroy Tinzelven.

I wouldn't normally kill a frog. Meet my pet frog Guu. (*show everyone Guu*). Sometimes he tells me about frog politics. But Gruung are not like Guu. They are evil. They are everywhere. And even though the Gruung stole my Gruung-skin cloak from me, all I know is that I have room in my hole for a new one.

Who here has fought a Gruung? (*wait for people to raise their hands*). Who here has fought a Gruung that could cast magic like a sister of the sisterhood?  have. Who has fought Gruung in the gold circle of Quaath? I have. Who has chased a Gruung through the gold circle until they went invisible like a coward? I have.

All I know is that SOMETHING is going on there. The Gruung are stronger than you think, but not stronger than me. I am the Gruungslayer. Also, please do not kill other frogs. It would make Guu sad.
