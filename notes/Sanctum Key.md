# Sanctum Key

Key of [[Pleetos Alentas]] - [[Sanctum Key]] 

Deep wells of conjuration, when inserted into the correct lock, it casts the spell Mordakinen's Magnificent Mansion. It will fit into many locks, but there's only one lock on the material plane where this will work. We're not sure if it will always open the same demi-plane or not.
