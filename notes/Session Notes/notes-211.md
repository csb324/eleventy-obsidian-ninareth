# Session 211

[[Freya]] and [[Molly]] arrive at [[The Rumble]]. After a brief moment of anticipation in the greenroom, [[Freya]] is escorted up to the arena to cheers of her name. She's facing a fucking dinosaur. [[Freya]] dives straight into it's mouth, fighting it from the inside, while an invisible [[Molly]] shoots it from the outside. 

They absolutely crush it, decimating the dinosaur and ending with [[Freya]] decapitating it from the inside, popping out head first from it's bleeding neck. 

At one point, during the fight, as the floor panels shift, they catch sight of a robed mage carrying a box that gives [[Freya]] a tingle about interplanar bs, while [[Molly]] seems able get a sense of the direction they go.  

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] reconvenes at the [[The Silver Tongue]]. When they tell us about the [[The Weaver]]'s tingles about the box, [[Freya]] asks [[Rawley]] if that was us or them -- it's them. 

On our way back, we run into [[Vask]] who tells us highlights from [[The Silver Halves]] adventure under the lake. They used a submersible, killed a bunch of fish people, raided a sunken ship, lost [[Magister]] for a day and half, but found the manifest they were looking for and some other treasure. [[Malachi]] did well and went to find [[Aisha]]. He agrees to hang around with us tomorrow for the big day, and in the short term watch as we go to the wayfinding competition to be sure no shenanigans happen while we're on the demiplane. 

We arrive at the [[Temple of Pelor in Thelran Asari]] and see a half circle of lights. People are popping in and out, most just tired, a few battered - all seem to heal slightly upon return. A slightly older librarian supervises the event, [[Davis Keln]]. 

Orb 8, we'll go into a pocket dimension, so no others are allowed ([[My Hole]] [[Bag of Holding]]). [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] tries to see if he's sus, but it doesn't seem like there's any specific note on where we were placed. [[Costi]] wants to check it out, but we can't touch it and any spells cast would disqualify [[Freya]]. [[Davis Keln]] says each year they employ both wayfinders and scholars at all of the academies to create hundreds of demiplanes, each tied to an orb. We enter, watched by an adjudicator, in our case [[Willah]]. We'll feel like we have two hours to do our task, if we're killed we will be popped out. Our task is to find our way to the signet ring. When we all touch it we'll be transported back. Scores are based on the time we're there but also our ability to reason through the various challenges and martial acumen. We divvy up what's in our [[Group Inventory]].

As we touch the orb and are transported, it seems like there's some sort of unexpected twist, a dark flash only [[Costi]] notices. We land in a meadow right about to begin sunset. There are mountains to the North, a dense forest to the East, rolling dunes to the South, and grassy plains to the West. First, we have [[Samuel]] scout from above. [[Freya]] finds a few symbols, one she doesn't remember, and one that is retired because it used to mean "go" but looked a lot like "dragons this way". We divide up to look for other symbols for [[Freya]] to interpret. The symbols we find: 

north - pits or treasure 
east - swinging ropes or hanging
south - retired go or dragons 
west - giant for food

We decide to go with the North, and make it a ways in before unfortunately catching ourselves in a pit trap. We all catch ourselves, no one gets caught. We debate if it's a full red herring, or if instead of an 'or' it's an 'and' scenario. Spotting a path at the bottom of the trap, [[Costi]] and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] who are flying decide to scout it out. They find evidence of a some creature down there, and we all nope out back to where we landed. 

In the main clearing, we try for a minute to be creative and look at/in/under the ground? It comes up with nothing. So we head south towards go-dragons. 

We walk through dunes for a while, before getting lured towards a harpy song at an oasis. [[Rez]] and [[Costi]] both decide we MUST go hang out and have a good time - but are both restrained (held and clotheslined respectively).
