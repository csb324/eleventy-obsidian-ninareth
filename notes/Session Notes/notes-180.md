# Session 180

As we begin our journey to the [[Bogwood Outpost]], [[Freya]] asks [[The Curator]] - does she know anything about [[woetouched]] places? Yes. Does she have anything to do with creating them? Seems like sorta? They tune in to where [[Freya]] is as she asks this. She does specifically bring up the [[Weaver's Needles]], [[The Curator]] doesn't seem to know what they are. Glad we told her! 

[[Freya]] asks if [[The Curator]] knows if the [[Everpresent]] is a person, or a group, but doesn't get an answer. [[The Curator]] answers that you have to be in a [[woetouched]] place in order to get [[The Shadowfell]]. [[The Curator]] agrees that for a favor, they can take [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] to [[The Shadowfell]]. 

[[Freya]] loops in [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] on the conversation, and her agreement to help us travel. [[The Curator]] isn't sure, but we don't know why - if it's our size, or something about one of us, or [[The Curator]]'s powers. 

We approach a raised drawbridge, with a small gatehouse on our side. [[Costi]] asks [[Malachi]] to go over and check out the other part of the bridge, [[Rez]] messages to warn him that we don't know we can trust him. [[Freya]] wants to join and tries to jump over and climb to meet them. As they go to move, [[Freya]] feels a deep sense of dread and just barely is able to stop [[Malachi]] as a giant bony hand claws [[Samuel]] down from the water, crushing him and dragging him underneath. 

We decide we should search the boathouse. Inside, most of it has been ripped up and destroyed, there's rotted stools, a skeleton lies on the floor, curled around a book. [[Freya]] and [[Hugh|Finn]] search inside, the rest of us look in from the door way. [[Hugh|Finn]] can tell the skeleton seems elven or human, and either recently dead or well-preserved. They were killed by a stab to the neck, through their spine. The book they're holding is [[Alliterative Argumentation: Tips and Tricks for Fancy Wordplay]]. 

The air cools, and the shadows from around us start to coalesce on the console, turning into 7 levers. Investigating, they are made of shadow but do have some sort of tangibility to them, but aren't fully solid. [[Aisha]] and [[Rez]] are able, there's a faint blue outline on each of them, and there is one of the bridge as well, but we only see it as [[Malachi]] draws near. 

[[Rez]] tries to push on why the shadows would change when [[Malachi]] comes near, as we all argue, he unbuttons his shirt to show a roped scar. [[Freya]] asks [[The Curator]], and she tells her the shadows are sketchy. [[Rez]] questions [[Malachi]] on why he's even asking. He says it's never healed, and when [[Freya]] asks if it sometimes feels cold we all pivot to what she's referring to. We all start fight, and [[Malachi]] seems to be the first to notice how out of character [[Aisha]] is acting and pulls her out for a heart to heart, apologizing to [[Rez]] for flirting with her girlfriend (he couldn't mean, [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]?) as he goes. 

[[Aisha]] is worried that we don't give a shit if they live or die, hates how [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] is treating [[Malachi]], and clearly doesn't trust us. [[Aisha]] spirals a bit about how maybe she's not actually supposed to be a [[Wayfinders]], and it's truly not supposed to be this hard. 

[[Malachi]] shares that he's been scared of the shadows since he came back, but the first time he hasn't felt that fear was when we just dispelled the [[Pelor scepter]]. He thinks this is the right path forward. [[Aisha]] worries that [[Malachi]] is clouded because he wants to fuck [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]. [[Rez]] tries to eavesdrop.

Meanwhile, [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] bickers, [[Costi]] telling off [[Hugh|Finn]] for trying to parent [[Rez]] and saying he needs to lay off. [[Hugh|Finn]] casts calm emotions and we all go down a notch. It helps us take a minute to look at room. After searching, and [[Rez]] trying to whisper alliterative phrases to the skeleton, we all grab a lever and pull.

Chaos. 

It turns out we were all cursed with fancy wordplay and having each other find out the curses allowed a part of the bridge to fall down, until we're all freed and able to cross towards the [[Bogwood Outpost]]. 

As we cross, [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] messages [[Malachi]] apologizing for it getting weird. He says he does things to push, but it seems like it was against their interest. He asks if [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] has seen any more effects, less obviously than whatever is up with [[Rez]].  

This prompts [[Malachi]] to ask [[Hugh|Finn]] if he could help people with not having their emotions tampered with. We all have a bit of a clear the air moment, [[Malachi]] warning [[Hugh|Finn]] that his kindness might make him too vulnerable here. [[Aisha]] says she's normal an optimist and not so suspicious. [[Rez]] apologizes to [[Malachi]] but does warn him that if he's going to try to steal [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], he'll lose - to which he replies that's not his style, but if [[Rez]] doesn't want to lose her she should make that clear. [[Hugh|Finn]] connects with [[Costi]], telling him he's proud of him and won't ever leave. We all seem a bit more balanced. 

We push through the doors to enter the [[Bogwood Outpost]]. Inside, it's a strange balance of still full of tables and vases, but in disrepair. 

A ghostly apparition walks through, an older human man, not in armor but nice clothing, with a small knife, with a bald head and short beard. He moves through the room straining and adjusting items fastidiously. He seems to be older than anything else we have seen. There's no clearly out of place object, and moving something away from where he placed it causes no reaction. 

Something is different about this ghost, compared to the others we've seen today. He was maybe more substantive? Or harder? 

We take the only clear path forward, a room into another large chamber on this level. There are dozens of books on the shelves, rotting arm chairs, a number of skeletons litter the ground but don't react. 

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] investigates the room, it seems there's some pattern to how things have aged but we can't make it out. [[Costi]] searches for any books still readable, and pulls one, [[The History of the Bogwood Outpost, c. 2680]], beginning with the construction, including blueprints. It ends 2750, and written by the [[Bogwood Outpost]]'s first Castellan [[Strictford]] - is a pretty straightforward accounting with no tragedy included. After he died, Castellan [[Lightpunch]] concluded the book. It doesn't feel redacted but it is intentionally worded. The banshee family we found, is mention, [[Colonel Skemf]]. 

It seems like the oppressive [[woetouched]] feeling is not as heavy or oppressive in here. We decide against any form of rest though, remembering how it altered our emotions last time. Pressing inward, we head into the banquet hall where we think there's a staircase down, it's filled with rotted food smell. The high ceilings are wreathed in shadow, but meat hooks appear to hang from them, blood drips down towards us. [[Freya]] is able to catch the marks where something was dragged from under the meat hooks into the basement. 

After a brief conversation, where [[Hugh|Finn]] expressed his concerns, we decide we have to go down to figure out if something, information or another item, waits for us in the dungeons. 

We reach a large iron door, ajar and unable to close, hearing an agonized screaming from inside. [[Malachi]] knocks the door open, and we head inside. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] once again uses her divine sense to find the source of the desecration, a small box on the ground, in the middle of the room. 

Our plan to trigger the vision and then shut the door backfires, as [[Freya]], [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], and [[Malachi]] are pulled into the room, where they see a halfling being mercilessly tortured, as they pierce out his eye. The older man, who's the ghost we saw earlier, comes out and begins to add to the torture. Our friends are pulled onto racks and begin to feel the torture as well. 

[[Rez]] makes a jack to in a panic try to open the door, [[Hugh|Finn]] helps pull it open. [[Aisha]] uses her humanculeus to bring the scalpel to the door for [[Costi]] to dispel as soon as he sees it. It works, and [[Rez]] sees the figment of [[Millik]] being tortured as they fade from view. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] runs to the ground to try and remove the final curse from the item. 

We leave the horrific area as fast as we can, and make our way to the door leading into the tower. Inside, we see mad ravings, formulas and scribbles written in blood on the walls. There are a few corpses, bound and gagged, on the ground. In the center of the room, there's a swirling black vortex, perhaps a permanent one, to [[The Shadowfell]].
