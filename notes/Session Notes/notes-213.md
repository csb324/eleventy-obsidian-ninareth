# Session 213

The fight continues to be bad. But little by little, we knock them out. We finally just eek it out, but all of their bodies are not from this plane. As our last enemy, the scythe guy, starts to go, she uses an ability from [[The Weaver]] to keep him here so we can try and take his stuff. This lets [[Costi]] have the chance to search his body. 

His body has at least 500 of these stones still on his body. Cutting the lines of tattoo at any point cause them to shatter. He has the signet ring we need for the [[Wayfinder Annual Gathering]] competition. [[Costi]] wristpockets the scythe to keep.

[[Rez]] and [[Freya]] determine the brass dragon has been dead for longer than a minute. [[Rez]] pours out the nice apricot brandy that she was hoping would be a party gift, and instead dissolves his body in the most potent acid she can create to spare him any potential mutilation from the [[Unscaled]]. 

As [[Freya]] touches the signet ring, we pop back out of the demiplane, to our watcher congratulating us and being in awe of actions and events that never took place. Sounds like at least we looked awesome though? The dragon they modeled our fake encounter off of was based on a historical dragon banished to another plane of existence during the first sundering. We learn we beat the record, at 26 minutes! We'll take it? 

We go back to the [[Platinum Sanctuary of Thelran Asari|Sanctuary of Saint Goldborn]] to take a short rest and recap before [[Freya]]'s speech. [[Freya]] tells [[Rawley]] what happened, mostly. [[Costi]] see's that his scythe is mundane, and that tracks somehow. He's particularly happy that we didn't let Scythe guy go back to his native plane, and hopefully took him off the map. [[Rawley]] is surprised. He asks us to write down as detailing of an accounting of what happened as is possible and put it into a lead box - [[Costi]] and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] are in it. We discuss how weird the attack was from a strategic point of the [[Everpresent]]. It does seem like the brass dragon was part of the demiplane and not real, to [[Rez]]'s relief. 

As we're walking to the [[Equinox Citadel]], [[Eirian|E]], [[Samson]], and [[Melnir]] are dressed up and walking to attend the speech too. [[Samson]] reminds us that the [[Home]]-contingent arrives tonight (because OF COURSE). 

Our whole contingent gets to the [[Equinox Citadel]]. Turns out, uh-oh, [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] is not invited to [[Freya]]'s dinner, which is apparently for [[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]] to introduce her to some of his friends. [[Molly]] sneaks his way in and thinks he's hidden. [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] is able to negotiate one person joining, at [[Freya]] taps in [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]. 

In the room: [[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]], [[Lirnis Kilzire]], [[Vy]], [[Demetri Jabokav]], [[Optan Stredvi]], [[Sotieu]]. There's three we don't recognize, a human ([[Nester Mallory]], Rep of [[The Peaks]] at the [[Thelran Asari Council]]), halfling ([[Dolores Dunde]] - Halfling, largest real estate holder in [[Thelran Asari]]), and hobgoblin ([[Esthek Pod]] native [[Thelran Asari]] military leader, Lt. Gen.) They ask for [[Freya]] to regale them with her wayfinding experience, and also what happened at [[The Rumble]]. We learn [[Jabokav]] got special tarot cards from [[Julian Slater-Runehorn|Julian]] he keeps in a golden box. He also teases that [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] might receive some offers, maybe the divination school for [[Costi]]. [[Optan Stredvi]] finds [[Freya]], they talk a little and he asks [[Freya]] to ride one of his horses to help with business tomorrow. He gifts her a violent stallion, and will send him to the [[The Silver Tongue]]. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] excuses herself, picking up that people were clearly holding back what they planned to say to [[Freya]]. [[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]] wants [[Freya]] to post in [[Thelran Asari]]. Seems like that was the point of the dinner. She gets soft offers of various types, it would be "first dibs" on the new planar wayfinding, and would need to decide within a month. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] comes to get [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]], gives us the run down. [[Rez]] and [[Vask]], with the help of [[Costi]], start rearranging the system to lead searches to books about sex. [[Costi's Dad|Vladdy Daddy]] shows up, and helps as we wait for our summons. 

Our now combined group goes to [[Freya]]'s pre-reception. None of the members of the [[Loombrace Cenacle]] are here. However, [[Julian Slater-Runehorn|Julian]] is (yay...) [[Davis]] is there, tells Freya that they've reviewed our wayfinding competition and will hand out laurel for the winners tomorrow and offers her a role on the Committee for next year. [[Freya]] also meets the President of [[The Rumble]], [[Elmster Duover]], he also offers her a perk, a residency there - like a weekly show for 6 months or a year. An eleven woman in [[Niraani Sisterhood]] robes introduced herself as [[Sorah Qualfiden]], asks if she'd like to depend her magical understanding - at least come for a few meetings. [[Rez]] asks [[Eirian|E]] what she knows about her, she's high ranking but not top. There's no reason to have suspicions of her yet but they share concerns if the [[Niraani Sisterhood]] has been breached. [[Eirian]] hopes to have some of the strongest from [[Home]] help tomorrow. 

[[Freya]] also clocks that [[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]] has an abjuration spell cast on him. [[Jabokav]] is riddled with magic, a couple of spells cast on him, his cloak is magic, his necklace and a few rings are as well. [[Vy]], [[Sotieu]], and [[Esthek Pod]] have dunamancy ping on them. 

[[Julian Slater-Runehorn|Julian]] corners [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], it's their normal dance of words. He asks if we'll be attending the auction tomorrow, [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] believes so but defers. [[Julian Slater-Runehorn|Julian]] will be heading to [[Arkhosia (City)]] after the Convention. We learn that [[Cliff Burbank]] is doing a tour to build goodwill with some of the other [[Three Dukes]], but the [[Burbanks]] are doing well in [[Eberron]]. [[Julian Slater-Runehorn|Julian]] is hoping to have a get together (for a week) at the [[Julian Slater-Runehorn|Slater-Runehorn]] country estate, with [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] and [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] as guests. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] asks about [[Jabokav]]'s tarot cards, [[Julian Slater-Runehorn|Julian]] brags that it took three adventuring parties - 10,000 gold and 12 lives - to acquire those cards. They significantly enhance the power of those who divine. He says be careful of having your future read with them, they make a lasting impact. 

[[Julian Slater-Runehorn|Julian]] inserts himself in a conversation with [[Costi]] and [[Costi's Dad|Vladimir Grosu]]. [[Costi's Dad|Vladdy Daddy]] gets a good read on him, and doesn't buy in and immediately backs [[Costi]], but does both keep things in line with they both drop hints about what they know. [[Costi]] holds it together though. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] asks [[Molly]] what [[Julian Slater-Runehorn|Julian]] smells like, and he answers tar.

And most concerningly, at the end of her speech, is hit with a compulsion to grab both sides of the podium - pricking each hand to bleed.
