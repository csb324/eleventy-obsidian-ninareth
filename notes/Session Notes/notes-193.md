# Session 193

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] heads towards the commotion, most of us running along the street and [[Molly]] casing the rooftops. 

As we round the corner, we see fleeing artists and the beginnings of a massacre. There's a group of Slaad, who kill several civilians. We do help encourage a new guard who is able to land the killing blow on a slaad. A green one, however, does get away. 

[[Costi]] has a conversation with the halfling that resisted his scatter and was saved by [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], who ends up being a little bit weird about it. He wants to leave, but maybe just doesn't feel good. [[Costi]] doesn't pick up anything weird immediately, but he does give him his address in case we need further conversations. [[Costi]] asks the guards to help detain him. [[Freya]] grabs the men who were scattered onto the roofs and brings them into the center of the space. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], [[Rez]], and [[Molly]] talk to the remaining guards. They said they were all getting drinks at a pub nearby. They said they entered the area after screams, and the cart was derailed and broken. Inside there's black glass lining the walls. They say there was originally about a hundred people in this square. 

[[Freya]] comes to check if there's anything trackable around the cart where the slaad escaped. They seemed to have been shackled within, but they were released. She does find tracks leading about 80 feet away, and a padlock that was neatly and professionally cut. It seems like the door might not have even been clearly open for a while. The tracks are small but in tough boots, by total coincidence [[Gimzid]] was wearing similar boots. Huh. 

[[Costi]] and [[Molly]] investigate the cart for signs of ownership, driver logs, or other information. It is branded to [[The Seven Copper Opera]], it was magically warded with anti-magic field that activates with the door is closed. One of the wrist shackles was intentionally damaged, the rest seemed to be done from within the cart. 

[[Freya]] reports in to [[Rawley]], he tells us it's slaad. He says we should quarantine the area, and clerics should cure disease asap, and the green can become other people. We learn we should cut the heads of the fallen but the average untrained person would meet bad ends in a couple of hours. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] heals several civilians. The red or blue can implant eggs that can take up to months to hatch. [[Ambrose]] agrees after talking to the [[Niraani Sisterhood]]. Anyone with power who turns is more likely to turn into the green. 

[[Rez]] sends to:
	[[Eirian|E]], "Soo, couple of slaad just attacked the Songside. Green got away. We probably need some clerics and guards and disease removals like now? Tell someone?". We tell the guards, including our new boy [[Ambrose]], who gets to work. 
		Um. Yeah, could have gone straight to the sanctuary with this one, Rez. We're on it though.
			You're the goody two shoes who reports to the Council, Sis! But don't worry, I got it - don't loop in E. Message received! 🙂
				In a crisis, you have my formal permission to go to the people who can ACTUALLY solve a problem first.

At [[Freya]]'s beckoning, [[Rez]] and [[Molly]] make a distraction so she can behead the bodies on sight without the guards interference. [[Costi]] turns the tied up slaad into a rabbit and we put him in a lead box. 

[[Rez]] suggests we cover up the words [[The Seven Copper Opera]] to be sure guards don't go straight there and connect to the guards to the [[Everpresent]]. [[Rawley]] says we should let them think they got away with it, he doesn't want them getting more careful. 

We take our prize slaad back to [[The Silver Tongue]], in the basement.
