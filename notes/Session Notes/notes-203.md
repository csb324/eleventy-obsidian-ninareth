# Session 203

As we re-arrive in a cleared and restored version of the lighthouse, we get the clear sense that we have returned to the [[Material Plane]]. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] brings [[Rez]] back from unconsciousness, [[Freya]] detects magic but finds the room still. [[Costi]] tries to investigate the pillars, nothing is jumping out at him. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], however, is able to tell the light is now consecrated again. 

We hear yelling from downstairs, and instead of deciding a cover story or how to deal with it, we decide to go into [[My Hole]] and sneak out. [[Jade]] makes herself and [[Freya]] invisible, and they carry us down. 

[[Freya]] lets [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] out and then they all climb up together. It's clear that they've cloistered all of the tourists together and aren't letting anyone leave. Several folks are injured, most are terrified. Guards are clearly blocking the way up to the top. 

[[Jade]] is able to read the lips of someone yelling that tell us darkness spilled out and covered people's eyes and they ran screaming. Also despite it being 3 minutes for us, it was 10 minutes for the [[Prime Material]]. 

[[Freya]] bugs [[Rawley]], no he can't tell if she travels interplanarly. She describes where we were, he's got a lot of ideas: a fringe part of [[The Shadowfell]], could be a necromantic wizard's demiplane, the are layers of [[The Abyss]] that might feel that way. 

As we stand their debating plan A) to run or plan B) something?? -- [[Costi]] notices approximately 30 guards are approaching up the hill. We panic and jump in [[My Hole]] again, which is baffling and also guilt-conveying but oh well! [[Freya]] invisibly clambers along the cliff side to try and flee. 

[[Jade]] overhears them saying that some people with smoke covering their eyes were all the way in THE CITY. uh-oh

[[Freya]] gets us back into [[Nervart]], but as we are all clambering out of [[My Hole]] a gaggle of tourists spots us. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] covers us like we were hiding from the danger, [[Costi]] goes into an excellent cover story about this relic of an old lobster market, [[Rez]] supports with a cover story manhole cover. We successfully diffuse the likelihood of them screaming. 

[[Rhynilex Svenik|Rhyn]] and [[Jade]] say they need to go tell [[No-Karo]], but they aren't fully sure where he is. They plan to leave at first light. They'll tell us what he says. 

We head back to the [[Wayfinders]] to get transported home. [[Dunc]] says shadow zombies were running through town. Like 5, they have not been captured yet. One was restrained at the [[Sourandi's Lighthouse]]. We ask [[Jade]] and [[Rhynilex Svenik|Rhyn]] to investigate and stop them. They agree. 

[[Dunc]] takes a suspiciously long time to get us sent on the teleportation circle. And then there's an equally suspicious pause as we are waiting to get let through. [[Rez]] is able to smell that we are in [[Thelran Asari]] but something is up. 

The door finally opens, and [[Rawley]], [[Lirnis Kilzire]], the [[Loombrace Cenacle]] [[Oyin Ifernell]], and [[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]] are here to welcome [[Freya]] to the [[Wayfinders]]. Desindever blows smoke up our asses, and his vibes are good natured (of course we know better). [[Rawley]] says he's proud of [[Freya]] (big eyes emoji). 

[[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]] (a.k.a. "Desi" but he prefers "superior desindever") asks to borrow [[Freya]] for a moment. On the way to the office, [[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]] asks if [[Freya]] would like to see a historical display to learn more about [[Wayfinders]] history. [[Freya]] both memorizes, and shortens, a way to his office. When they reach his office, it's gorgeous inside. Notably, there's a big portrait behind the desk that immediately registers with her memory of [[Kith and Kin]]. It shows a group of people, at the front is the first Superior of the [[Wayfinders]], [[Valoras Cinknotae]], leading a delegation from [[Thelran Asari]] into [[Durmask]] in the first journey after [[The Second Sundering]]. It's mostly normal, though he closes by saying she will be pulled in certain directions but he wants her to go down the 'right' path, claiming she always has a friend in his office.

When she asks for tips on how to make the most of the [[Wayfinder Annual Gathering]] they recommend the gauntlet and the wayfinding conversation. [[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]] says he'll have a list of some people he thinks she should talk to. [[Freya]] fails a wisdom saving throw on the way out, not that any of us know that. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] can see [[Rawley]] is playing it cool but strung tight. He tells us all to be chill. He shares about [[Freya]]'s surprise party tonight, and [[Rawley]] gives us all a yellow topaz sun [[Wayfinder Guest Pin]] so that we can move about the [[Equinox Citadel]]. He asks to gather in the [[Twilight Sanctuary]] for the party. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] and [[Rez]] take a short rest in a library, avoid talking about lingering unanswered points, but [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] does pull out the book [[The Kfalgaarder]]. It's about this man who like, over the course of 700 pages, is fired over a middle management dispute and convinces himself that 1) the most virtuous thing a man can do is support his family, 2) he has failed, 3) that’s his family’s fault, for needing support, 4) the world would be better without him, because of his failure, and 5) his family couldn’t survive without him, because he supports them, so, 6) he should kill his family and then himself. and he does! it’s a REALLY dark book. Dwarvish literature is CRAZY. The mountains do something to them psychologically I think. So he keeps “going to work” and he just spends all day thinking about how much he hates himself and the only way out is to murder everyone — and the whole book is this internal monologue of this man going slowly insane under the pressure of like, masculinity and status — but he convinces himself it’s out of love. it’s SO fucked. It’s beautifully written but like, probably the most fucked up thing I’ve ever read.
