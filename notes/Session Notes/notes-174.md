# Session 174

We head to the [[Platinum Sanctuary of Thelran Asari]] alongside [[Zmaj]]. The whole building is made of winding layers of the five metallic colors, rising up almost 300 ft at the highest points. The whole [[Plaza of the Faithful]] is packed with people in the morning. [[Zmaj]] is feeling a little less confident, and defers to [[Rez]] - though he's also proud of her? And wants to see a bit of her growth. [[Rez]] is convinced he's making her lead so that she has to be impossible. 

[[Rez]] knows the current leader of this temple is the [[Gold Scale]] [[Fimbeth Udovarax]], a red and blue dragonborn, who was recently appointed after his predecessor retired. She says [[Gold scale Topaz]] sent them. A halfling receptionist, clearly not buying it, she humors them to go and get her boss, a fading black scale dragonborn, [[Brass Scale Zusk]]. He recognizes the code phase, and maintaining his grumpy demeanor, takes them to a back holding room. 

For the next hour, we entertain ourselves. [[Hugh|Finn]] knits (it was not a group invitation), [[Zmaj]] does his morning forms, [[Freya]] tries to follow him, [[Costi]] tries to shadow him with a mirroring silent image. [[Rez]] looks around, following [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]'s bad vibes hunch, to very carefully (poorly) assess the room. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] tries to find any religious imagery. 

[[Gold Scale Udovarax]], with some guards, escorts us through a secret passage to his offices. They're a large sunlit octagonal office. [[Freya]] and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] are on HIGH alert. Through the windows, we can see into the dome, where we see the Statue of Saint Goldborn, near life size and make of solid gold crafted into scales, enchanted into suspending over the ground. He explains it took 80 years to make. 

Before we start, we ask him to go under a zone of truth - he agrees, and asks if we will as well. Only [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] resists. [[Zmaj]] lets it take effect. 

He says a few months ago that the [[Gold Scale]]s received a missive from the [[Order of the Platinum Dragon]] saying [[Rez's Family]] might be in a danger. [[Rez]] gives him the rundown on the [[Unscaled]]. He says most in high society in [[Thelran Asari]] would know of [[Pleetos Alentas]] by name, at least. [[Gold Scale Udovarax]] says he'll try to track down where [[Pleetos Alentas]] is, if possible. 

Then we pivot to telling about [[Wait There's a Lot of Undead]] and [[There's Plainly Something Wrong with the Planes]]. [[Gold Scale Udovarax]] says [[Gulphi Delvinson]] is a laughing stock, often raving about insane things, and [[Fimbeth Udovarax]] is really having a hard time believing it. He agrees we should call a [[Priest Moot]], but says it cannot be allowed for [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] to attend. After some reflection [[Fimbeth Udovarax]] says he would not trust [[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]] with more private information. He considers [[Umenelis Wocornalkint]] a close friend. 

[[Costi]] clocks that [[Fimbeth Udovarax]] hopes to one day have the strength to create more metallics, though would not, because it was forbidden. He also thinks we're (metallics) delightful! 

He say the [[Priest Moot]] will take about a week, and [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] will meet up afterwards.  He offers specific questions for [[Bahamut]] to let him know. We learn he uses a special item, the [[Dragon Heart Staff]], he was given when he became head of the sect, in order to cast divine magic.

[[Zmaj]] brings up his discomfort with the ways that the gods played mortals against each other in the [[Heal the Anchor Tree]], and [[Fimbeth Udovarax]] says that gods that are good or evil are so on a macro scale, but that sometimes their plans don't make sense to us, and on the small scale, they might be cruel. And for many, they were all once men (or dragons). 

He also thinks we're ([[The Unforged Few|Our Party]]) delightful! 

We leave to go and see the Statue of Saint Goldborn. There's smaller statues of all of [[Rez's Family]], [[Zmaj]] and [[Rez]] take a moment beside [[Nehushta|Neti]] and [[Zoheret|Zoha]]. At the end, [[Zmaj]] takes a quiet moment, saying "I could really use you right now Dad".  [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] glowers at it. [[Rez]] will try and make the statue a bit more [[Rez's Dad|Azir]], and have it wink a few kids. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] has a minor meltdown trying to get alone at the [[Rooted Grove]], but oops [[Rez]] is with her. [[Zmaj]] escorts [[Hugh|Finn]] to his temple, and [[Freya]] goes to buy potions with [[Costi]] - buddy system! 

[[Hugh|Finn]] talks to [[Zmaj]] about what it means to be in a hoard, he shares it's colloquial, a mindset more than anything - he considers his troops his hoard. [[Zmaj]] notes that it's easy to forget humanoids don't like that, and [[Rez]] hasn't spent as much time with people. [[Hugh|Finn]] shares that he does not like the idea of being owned. 

[[Hugh|Finn]] goes and settles down at one of the hearths. He tries to communes with [[TrueSilver]]:
1. Did [[Moradin]] reject me because I am…a creation?
	    No
2. Can you direct me towards someone who would be helpful to spread your teachings here?
	    No, you're doing that already.
3. Do you still eat favorite foods?
		Yes!

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] reluctantly leads [[Rez]] towards the [[Rooted Grove]], to talk to [[Umenelis]]. She gives her the heads up that the [[Priest Moot]], and [[Umenelis]] notices something is wrong -- not that [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] would EVER open up. This is news to [[Rez]] who also tries to ask what's up, it's even less successful. Noticing [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] is trying to lose her, [[Rez]] 'lets' her. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] finally lets her guard down and pours her heart into her harp. [[Rez]] is an idiot. Even so, she gets enough to know this is fucking SAD. She's going to ask [[Eirian]] if she's heard of those songs. 

[[Costi]] and [[Freya]] buy potions at [[Krystal]], they get 5 superior, then convince the store owner to give them experimental potions to be tested on. [[Costi]] says he'll document symptoms if they knock off some cost. They land on we pay them 200gp if it works and they'll give us 300gp if it doesn't.
