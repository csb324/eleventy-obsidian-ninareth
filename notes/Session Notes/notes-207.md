[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] coordinates a greater restoration for [[Molly]] at the [[Corellon Larethian]] temple.

[[Costi]], [[Freya]], and [[Molly]] are going to imperceptible [[Molly]] to do some light recon in the [[Equinox Citadel]]. After casing some options, [[Freya]] cast weaver's shroud on [[Molly]]. He goes to investigate the space where the speeches will be taking place. [[Molly]] first goes to investigate the podium. The first row of seats are 60 feet away from the podium. There's nothing notable on the podium, but [[Molly]] breaks the riser so [[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]] can't be seen well. Going into the restricted lower section, [[Molly]] looks around and catches the scent of stone that is different than the surrounding area, and finds an illusory wall. Traveling in, he follows stairs down to below the ground. Breaking traps and dispelling magic and picking the lock, he finds a chamber with elaborate runes on the entire floor. A large room is on the other side, interlocking all of the runes are hundreds of overlapping circles like the [[Freya's Mysterious Circles|Eye of the Everpresent]]. Every 3 or so feet there are deposits in glass bulbs of a silvery substance, people in hazmat hoods are actively filling them and putting them in place. The room is illuminated by torches and the ceiling is 40 feet high. 18 constitution check. [[Molly]] takes note of one of their voices.

They do have a cursed sewing needle that creates a death-like sleep. They also have a [[Stone of Misfortune]] that brings harm to whoever is in physical contact with it. Or we could sovereign glue it to them. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] offers to let us store it in their vaults to help with the risk profile. [[Stone of Misfortune]] is 2,000, and the [[Sovereign Glue]] is 1,000, the finders fee is another 2,000. Any solvent's we use will be 1,700. She's going to take all our money just in case, but we'll get most of it back. We've got a deal. We get a greater healing potion on the house.
