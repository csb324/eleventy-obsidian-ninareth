# Session 196

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] plans what to do in our down time. 

[[Costi]] spends some time, with the tenuous help of [[Molly]], to right a con piece for the [[Thelran Asari Society]]. He frames the post as immigrants taking jobs, but slowly building their humanity and eventually concluding with a point on their own struggles. The threat of [[Molly]] eating his papers spurs [[Costi]] to rethink into a pretty good direction. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] wants to find a longer term, traditionally trained, swordsman to help prep her [[The Order of the Blue Rose]] after we have to eventually leave. [[Freya]] suggests we go to [[The Rumble]] and find a [[Wayfinders]]. [[Rez]] tags along. 

The [[Frontier]] is in horrible upkeep, roads are haphazardly built, and we get a bit lost. Eventually though we find our way. 

After seeing some sights on the way in, we meet [[Crumb]], a lizardfolk, and tell him we might be interested in fighting after watching, and he pulls us aside for a chat. He earns commission and reeeeally wants us to turn in the paperwork. We take our forms and decide to maybe fill it in while watching. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] uses the time to tee up a conversation she's been waiting on with [[Freya]], and asking about her seemingly changed fighting styles. [[Freya]] opens up that during her pledging to the [[Loombrace Cenacle]] she could choose to go to [[The Curator]] or [[The Weaver]], and she chose a warlock pact with [[The Weaver]]. We try to get some clearer info but [[Freya]]'s operating mostly on vibes. She thinks she has a better read of the [[Planes of existence]] now. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] asks that she just tell [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] next time. 

We recognize [[Skiv]], [[Nekto]], and [[Durembur]] of [[The Burning Sons]], without [[Rieltar]]. Unfortunately [[Freya]] is immediately spotted. We awkwardly discuss what to do, and decide to tell [[Costi]] and [[Molly]] to come on the quicker side, [[Rez]] sends - 

"Rumbles are so cool! Lots of stuff to see. Like a monkey, and great mushroom bites and OH - THE BURNING SONS. Y'all should come. Now?", [[Costi]] responds, "Awwww shit, okay. Coming."

When [[Costi]] learns of and questions [[Freya]]'s pact, she messages [[Rawley]] for back up -- turns out he's shocked and never heard of that sort of relationship. Uh-oh. One more thing for us to discuss. 

We find [[Crumb]] again, and give him our forms. He leads us to the under section, and the staff there are shocked - but do allow him to give us the tour. Beneath the building, the is a tunnel that surrounds the entire multi-block building, cafeterias, training rooms, and dorms for those who fight or work. Underneath the arena itself, the monsters are kept. 

We pass by [[The Burning Sons]] and while they don't confront us, [[Molly]] is able to read [[Skiv]] holding back one of them, saying "not now [[Nekto]]". 

Another group also notices us, a very beefy and large [[Wayfinders]] orc with spiked geometeric dyed orange hair. [[Rawley]] tells us its [[Vy]], [[Freya]] fakes and learns she's the runner up of the [[Wayfinders]] wreath last year. She's based in [[Thelran Asari]] at the Headquarters. She says [[Rawley]] has a reputation as a has-been who wasted his potential, and is now going to see if he's proved himself with his protege or not. 

We learn that [[Crumb]] used to be allowed but isn't anymore. He went to jail for scalping off the top and just recently got out. He's BIG fans of ours now. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] does circle back to the female fighter she tracked earlier, a half-elf named [[Draetha]] (yes, she's from [[Ru'Nileth]]). [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] asks if she's looking for work, and shares about the group she's trying to train. She's quite direct, they agree to a rate of 12 gp / week. They agree she will begin in the morning. 

[[Costi]] sendings to [[Aisha]]: Aish, we’ve met a crazy creature who calls itself a “MoggleWoggle” or Molly. We learned he’s a thing called a Mimsy! Supposedly he is actually a dream of a powerful being made material.. somehow? Anything you can learn about these things would be appreciated. I think you’d find him fascinating—hopefully you can come meet him soon. Oh, also Finn is taking some much needed time off. Luckily Molly is shockingly good with a hand crossbow.
