[[Rawley]] arrives and we immobilize the slaad to tie him up for interrogation

We learn slaad are beings of chaos who live on a plane called [[Pandemonium]] on  [[The Abyss]]. [[Molly]] sets his mind link into the slaad. [[Rawley]] says they arrived a few months back, suddenly appeared with 3 other slaad. Kept in the basement, fed periodically, but no ability to reproduce. This morning, loaded into a crate. Green one is good at common. They were being taken north to an arena, likely [[The Rumble]], a series of gladiatorial contests.

[[Molly]] tries to speak to it, says its God/mother. Asks some deep questions. Slaad lived in a house with 19 rooms, including a hat room. His name is [[Burv]]. [[Green Slaad]] is [[Vurb]]. [[Burv]] doesn't know [[Gimzid]]. Prison also had a [[Skittering Horror]]. Freya kills the slaad.

We determine there's likely another amount of slaad who've now been created in the city. Transformation happens in a burst of gore, most humans change in 24 hours but strong adventurers could take months. Different colors of slaad also contribute. There's little known about reproductive process of green, because they're the result of powerful creatures turned by a red or blue. They can take the shape of a humanoid relatively immediately. They can speak common fine but can't gain memories or voices. Gray cannot reproduce.
