# Session 183

We quickly disguise ourselves, [[Aisha]] as [[Adrith Cornsyth]], [[Malachi]] as the orc woman, and [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] as [[Everpresent]] generic dudes. 

Beginning our journey, [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] uses the [[Wolfskull Sage's Ring]] to give us a much much needed boost. Sneaking through [[The Outpost]], we pass several very large landscapes. They give us a deep sense of unease, and several of us ([[Rez]], [[Malachi]], [[Freya]], and [[Aisha]]) get caught contemplating them.

[[Rez]] is caught in a flashing round of hunger - experiences she craves, time with her whole family, unbroken together, [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] draped out before her, images of other past lovers, new and unique, but all not yet real - empty but so close.

[[Malachi]] stares up at a portrait, the exact look on his father's face as he first awoke after the murder of their family. The grief that separated them slowly over time, leading to the near loss of their relationship too, but without the cover of love - just bottomless grief. 

[[Aisha]] sees spiraling arcane equations, similar to those on the [[Everpresent]]s portal design, but more. Unlocking hidden knowledge, pushing even further, she can learn more than any others.

[[Freya]] pictures herself dangling over a black bottomless void, fingers nearing touching the [[Cenacle Oak Box]], slipping as she stretches, but knowing if she just pushes a little further,  a little more, maybe then.

As [[Malachi]] and [[Freya]] are able to break out of this trance, the images for [[Aisha]] and [[Rez]] become clear to everyone else. The two of them reach to touch the paintings, dropping unconscious. In their minds, they both go from hungrily watching these images, to living in the situations, experiencing them, despite not being fulfilled. 

Everyone awake tries to break the hold from the paintings. Healing doesn't work, nor does dumping water (thanks [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]). [[Costi]] studies the magical tie, and it seems to be feeding on them, leaching something from them. [[Freya]] and [[Malachi]] start to attack the paintings. 

As the images start to waiver, losing the objects of their deepest desire, [[Rez]] and [[Aisha]] desperately trying to cling to their gains. [[Costi]] recognizes that it's pulling magical energy from them, alongside necrotizing their bodies. 

Panicking, [[Malachi]] cradles [[Aisha]], begging her to let it go and come back, while [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] begs [[Rez]] too -- but to get it together, and lock it the fuck in. 

They both finally break free, the paintings showing the final crescendo of hunger, but twisted - [[Rez]]'s shows a door slamming closed, with a cold, disaffected [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] turning away on the otherside. [[Aisha]]'s flickers through a number of experiments, blood on the walls, before ending at bright white late. 

[[Rez]] protests, saying it was all so real, she just had it - [[Freya]] tries to tell her it all wasn't real, [[Hugh|Finn]] tells her it can be, she locks into that. 

[[Malachi]] scoops up [[Aisha]], bringing her down the hallway, pulling her away from the temptation. As they leave, [[Aisha]] remembers some of what she learned, parts of it were real. [[Rez]] remains an idiot. 

We make our way through hallways, before getting stopped by a ghostly woman asks [[Jahlrak]] why he's not at his post. He calls her Mistress, and says he's escorting new living to the Castellan to test their loyalty. She says she felt the emotions from the gallery. We convince her that we belong, she says she'll go skim some of what's left in the paintings. Her name is [[Lissia]], she's loyal to the Castellan. 

Continuing sneaking, we make it to the banquet hall, we're warned to move forward with our heads down, but to answer if spoken to, and to disrespect the nobles.

On stage, a kobold band is recently skewered on meat hooks, bleeding out on the ceiling. As we try to make our way though, a decaying obese noble, [[Lord Devvis]], who sits at the head of the table spots us - demanding to know what we're doing here. [[Freya]] jumps in, staying she's starving, and surprisingly, it hits the man just the right way. Laughing, he brings her up to the table and tells the rest of us to get up on stage. [[Freya]] keeps the act up, almost outing herself calling out the bugs, which are unknown and insulting him. She learns he's Lord Regent of the Outpost. [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] make our way to the stage, [[Rez]], [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], and [[Malachi]] pull out instruments, [[Hugh|Finn]] does the warforged stomping dance, while [[Aisha]] and [[Costi]] bring out magical images of dancers. Thankfully, we put on a hell of a show. [[Lord Devvis]] says we should have rooms made up to keep on entertaining the court, allowing us to leave. [[Freya]] stomachs the world's worst wine, and makes a friend. 

Escaping, we continue our way out, traveling through the study we encountered on the way in. Three humanoid-ish figures are inside, a hungry, but for knowledge, plus two spiked and twisted figures pulling each other closure in desperation in the corner. 

We clear out the creatures, ending with [[Aisha]]'s gun popping out multiple barrels and killing the remaining three at the same time. Casing the book shelves, before she could stop herself, she grabbed a large red-bound leather book off the shelf. [[Freya]] warns her all, and especially this one, gives off necromantic energy. 

[[Aisha]] opens it, with a deep red infernal writing beginning to appear. She stops breathing, and blood drops from her eyes onto the book. [[Malachi]] and [[Freya]] force the book shut, and lecture her on stopping looking for information here. 

Just as we start to leave, Castellan [[Strictford]] comes up and greets us. Asks if we enjoyed his hospitality, and immediately clocks why we're really here - mentioning seeing all of [[Rez]]'s deepest desires. He says he will not notify the [[Everpresent]] to our arrival, if we promise to make their deaths memorable -- to help cement some of the columns he has put in place here. He claims that he can see, if he tries, anything that happens in [[Bogwood Outpost]]. 

We say that is doable, but if we want to put on a good show, we need to take a nap - [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] all comes together to try to persuade him to let us long rest in [[Bogwood Outpost|The Outpost]]. He ponders, says it's too much risk to him to let us stay, but agrees to lower the bridge, and after a few minutes, brings up a box, with 5 vials. Tells us to pick 3. 

superior hp - true
light blue silver flecks
thick green viscous - poison, fairly potent
glittery silver 
superior hp - poison, fairly potent

We leave the poison and take the rest. [[Castellan]] [[Strictford]] grabs [[Jahlrak]] for a muffled conversation before we leave, mostly talking, and [[Jahlrak]] agrees. 

As we head out, the bridge lowers for us. There are many shades walking about, some obvious, some more subtle. Every few minutes we see something wrong, twisting a mundane object or normal activity, into a horror for a moment. 

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] does our best to blend in as we make our way towards the farm house. Hearing nothing on our approach, we walk around to the backyard where the graves had been placed on the [[Prime Material]]. [[Freya]] tries to hand her the hair pin, and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] persuades her that we found something of hers. As soon as she touches it, she begins weeping -- placing the hair pin over her broken skull, replacing some bone there. She invites us inside for tea. 

[[Deliah Skempf]] says she's been keeping her head done, tending to her family. She's noticed more living than normal, but really only the [[Castellan]] comes around, mostly to sit and watch. 

We help her bury her family and share a cup of tea. Adrienne asks if she has a recommendation for somewhere we could rest, and she offers to let us sleep here. 

She's heard grave dirt can offer some protective properties and circles the two houses. [[Costi]] alarms the doors and windows. He asks if she wants to stay in [[The Shadowfell]], saying it's not the home where her family lived, it's instead a prison. She's taken aback, and doesn't know where else she would go. He shares that by nature of her suffering, the [[Castellan]] and others are using her. They are likely to seek the re-desecration of her and her hairpin. He wishes nothing more than to give her options, but the only one available to him is to offer her an end. She's taken aback, but asks to sleep on it. [[Costi]] says there's only a few who have the ability to help this realm, but it comes at great personal cost. [[Deliah Skempf]] notes that life is not very fair, but she'll think about it. 

Meanwhile, [[Hugh|Finn]] talks to [[Jahlrak]], who shares the [[Castellan]] offered him a deal: if he creates an anchor to replace the one lost in the scalpel, [[Strictford]] will not pursue him. [[Hugh|Finn]] does his best to convince him there's another way, [[Jahlrak]] says he will not subject another to what he's experienced. 

[[Deliah Skempf]] asks [[Jahlrak]] to talk. [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] debates their future, how autonomous they are, landing on the fact that their journey into the silence awaiting must be their own choice. 

[[Freya]] and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] take first watch together. They bitch about [[Rez]]. being bitchy but agree [[The Shadowfell]] is tough on everyone. [[Freya]] tells about her experience yearning in the painting, side tracking that she desperately wants to get the [[Cenacle Oak Box]], and doesn't know how to get the answer she needs without it. She trusts [[Rawley]] and thinks he is what led her on this path. If she can't trust him, she doesn't know how she could trust anyone in any capacity again. 

They circle back on [[Rez]]'s painting ending on [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] closing the door. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] is surprised but immediately hides it and tells [[Freya]] they are only friends, and need to stay that way. Eventually opens up, saying [[Rez]] couldn't see it the way they can. [[Freya]] shares that [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] excites her because he views things the way she does, and counts her as part of what he cares about most. They share a fundamental difference of what a hoard is, and then whether or not it is possible, truly, for it to be more than casual. [[Freya]] argues yes, [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] thinks there is no way for it to work. [[Freya]] tries to reassure her she's in control of her emotions, and if it turns bad, she can always decide it later if she needs it. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] asks [[Freya]] if she thinks what she had with [[Ronan]] was real, or if he was always in on it. She says it depends on the day. If he was always planning to betray her then there's nothing to salvage, even on her own. It's where her rage comes from. She acknowledges the drinking is part of that, she needs ways to cope. They bicker about how important [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] is to [[Rez]] until their watch ends. 

[[Malachi]] and [[Aisha]] take a watch together. [[Malachi]] tells her she might have a hard choice to make, that he cannot be made into a battery he cannot be kept here. She deflects, saying he shouldn't be a martyr and he shouldn't be so ready to die but he pushes. He gets emotional, saying he cannot be here for 200 years. And asks for her promise - if it's between leaving him or putting him down, that she will help him. [[Aisha]] only agrees that she'll do what needs to be done, and no more. They reach an understanding, but only after [[Aisha]] reminds [[Malachi]] that [[Malachi's Dad]] would miss him too.

They also, of course, discuss the paintings as well. [[Aisha]] connects that [[Rez]] is weird on some core level that she doesn't really understand. [[Malachi]] talks about the glory of a 40sq/ft nude [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]. Then [[Aisha]] relates back to what she saw, and if she could learn enough about this magic, maybe there wouldn't be a risk to [[Malachi]]'s life. He says theory is not what is imminently possible. 

[[Hugh|Finn]] pulls [[Rez]] for a watch, telling her he thinks she hurt [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]'s feelings with the portrait. [[Rez]] says that can hardly be her fault, it was her unconscious being pulled out of her, showing things she wants and things she's wanted. [[Hugh|Finn]] lectures her on not toying with [[Rez]], who tries to explain that [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]'s in control here - she's the one saying no, pulling away, and [[Rez]] has been TRYING. [[Hugh|Finn]] agrees to watch [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] for her reactions, and suggests a more traditional wooing of her. [[Rez]] tries to learn more about that. [[Hugh|Finn]] ends by sharing his fear of tomorrow, [[Rez]] agrees, but says she trusts in his healing. [[Hugh|Finn]] says he worries [[Freya]] won't want to leave, [[Rez]] says she trusts that [[Freya]] knows [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] is helping her get the answers she needs. 

In the morning, [[Costi]] asks [[Deliah Skempf]] and [[Jahlrak]] their thoughts. They agree they are not ready to be done yet. He offers to kill [[Dolrak]] once we're through the portal, and then he will protect [[Deliah Skempf]] and any of the other people we free.
