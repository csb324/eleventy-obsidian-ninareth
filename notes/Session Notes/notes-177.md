# Session 177

[[Costi]] tries to find another old woman to bankroll his gambling, but mostly just conspicuously walks around eyeing grandmas. 

[[Aisha]] pulls [[Malachi]] for a private chat, noting some displeasure in [[Aisha]]. She's upset [[Malachi]] isn't playing nice, especially given that [[Freya]] was there. She wants this (whatever that is). [[Malachi]] apologizes, but is distrustful of [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] and knows they don't have all of the information. [[Malachi]] says he can trust a cynic though, referring to [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], to which [[Aisha]] dismisses it as she's hot. [[Aisha]] insists she really wants this, and needs [[Malachi]] not to ruin it for her. He agrees, so long as she agrees to cut and run if [[Malachi]] senses trouble before they find all the answers she seeks. 

After their chat, [[Malachi]] tries to copy [[Costi]] into conning old men, while [[Aisha]] tried to pick his pocket -- she's stopped by [[Melnir]], who said because she walked in "with the Master's sister" they'd get one warning but makes it very clear there's no stealing tolerated in the [[The Silver Tongue]]. 

[[Costi]] finds them to chat afterwards, eventually clarifying that [[Rez]]'s sister owns [[The Silver Tongue]]. [[Malachi]] is thrilled to hear that she got caught. They all agree to blow off some time drinking together. 

[[Freya]] goes to say goodbye to [[Zalvaris|Zalv]], and finds him packing up all of his weapons. He's going to [[Dilandil]] to soldier under [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] for a bit. They talk for a bit, then "say goodbye" for longer. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] and [[Hugh|Finn]] go to find [[Zmaj]]. He shares he'll be back in [[Dilandil]], but doesn't know where - it'll depend on his orders. She asks if he's ever crossed paths with her brother, [[Zmaj]] hasn't, but can. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] shares he won't talk to her, at least unless she travels to [[Ru'Nileth]], which is impossible for the obvious reasons, but asks if he can validate why she can't make it there. He agrees on one condition, [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] writes a letter, and [[Zmaj]] will give it to him when he thinks he's ready. [[Zmaj]] asks her to keep [[Rez]] safe. [[Hugh|Finn]] asks if he can help transport us, and [[Zmaj]] shares about his extensive planning on mapping a network of trees. He can get us to an area about a day and a half from the [[Bogwood Outpost]]. They talk about [[Vask]]'s brief stint as a [[Wayfinders]], saying it only lasted a month and a half, and ended in much bloodshed. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] leaves to write a letter. 

[[Hugh|Finn]] rallies a tipsy gathering of [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] along with [[Aisha]] and [[Malachi]]. [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] and [[Zmaj]] are there to greet us, and is surprised by our guests. [[Malachi]] enters into a pissing match he loses with [[Zmaj]], [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] demonstrates [[Mr. FIx-It]] by stabbing him (he gets better).

After seeing through a prank illusion of an alarmingly large dicked halfling following [[Malachi]], we travel through the willow tree behind [[The Silver Tongue]] and make it into the [[Narathian Plains]]. Saying goodbye to the brothers, [[Zmaj]] warns [[Rez]] to be careful and has a pointed moments, [[Rez]] has not forgotten. [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] stakes his claim again on [[Freya]], through both a large loud kiss, and gives her paper with his own sigil on it - telling her it can be a brand next time if she wants it. [[Rez]] is annoyed, and [[Rez]] will also miss them. 

The three [[Wayfinders]] make a bit of game to see who can get us there best, [[Malachi]] wins. [[Costi]] starts to have some confusing feelings. We make camp about a day's travel from [[Bogwood Outpost]]. 

We play a game of What the Fuck is Up Is That? [[Aisha]] shares the note she received:

* [[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]] is not to be trusted
* The Shadows Gather
* Something is happening the year [[3454]] (two years from now) 
* Something ties together the [[Everpresent]], an [[Freya's Mysterious Letter|Eye of the Everpresent]], Time and Space
* [[Oghma]] and the [[Cult of Knowledge]] are implicated
* Beware Those with Hands of Gold [[Alhoon]]

[[Malachi]] asks why the rest of [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] cares. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] talks about her death, we mention [[Heal the Anchor Tree]] and we all share that our gods are tied in. We learn [[Aisha]] graduated from the [[Veraxyes College]]. They escorted a group to [[K'falgaard]], but don't really know why they were scouted to the [[Loombrace Cenacle]]. He thinks none of us know why we're involved, and we all hedge because we do. [[Freya]] shares about [[Synchrona]] and [[Polaris]]. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] notices that [[Malachi]] starts to shutdown after mentions of [[The Shadowfell]], and [[Aisha]] ends the conversation. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] specifically brings up that we'll have to be interacting with [[The Shadowfell]], and wonders if everyone will be okay with that. [[Aisha]] says she trusts [[Malachi]] with her life, and with ours, and if he can't handle something he will tell her. 

[[Malachi]] takes a walk to cool down with [[Hugh|Finn]], and after a tense 15 minutes, asks if [[Hugh|Finn]] really can scry on people. He describes a drow in [[Durmask]], named [[Favenir]], and gives him a burl-wood pocketknife. 

A workshop appears, showing an older drow with tight haircut, strong arms, working on a fine armoire. He's doing fine detailing, and [[Hugh|Finn]] is able to recognize a resemblance to [[Malachi]]. 

[[Aisha]] asks what the fuck is up is with that to [[Samuel]], [[Costi]] hints that he doesn't fully know what [[Our Fiend Samuel]] is, but he mysteriously gets some powers and is connected to [[The Raven Queen]]. 

[[Malachi]] comes back, somewhat angry at our flippant conversation. He says we might not actually know what's on the line, and while we disagree, he asks who's actually been to [[The Shadowfell]]. [[Rez]] says she has, and had a great time. [[Malachi]] doesn't understand how that's possible,  [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] says [[Rez]] is a unique case. [[Rez]] asks [[Malachi]] to share more, and he asks for [[Freya]]'s bathtub liquor as a balm to the conversation. 

A drunk [[Malachi]] shares that as a teenager, in [[The Underdark]], he was starved, thrown to a pack of wolves, and tortured. In [[Durmask]], he made his way into [[the Pits]], and got trapped in a pit of [[The Shadowfell]]. He doesn't remember how he got out. [[Rez]] tries to reassure him by sharing that [[Kaycee Teapetal|Rez's Mom]] could help if that get into trouble, but it doesn't seem to land well. He spends a lot of time fiddling with a red stone on him. 

Before bed, [[Aisha]] tries to help [[Hugh|Finn]] with installing his new wrist. She begins investigating, reassuring him she'll do her best to handle this learning with care. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] lays on hands to cure his drunkenness and "bring him back to the horrors"

We plan watches, [[Hugh|Finn]], then all the [[Wayfinders]], then [[Rez]] and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]. 

[[Hugh|Finn]] notices the shadows are a touch too long. During that time, [[Malachi]] wakes with a start. He notices [[Aisha]] sitting at the fire, and when he approaches, she asks him for help with her gun. Confused, he holds the weapon while she moves, and from behind, a long limbed figure grabs him from behind. All of [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] rise with shadows for eyes and [[Aisha]] says "the shadows will always be with you". Shaken, he cannot go back to sleep. 

[[Rez]] and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] have a chat during their watch, she warns [[Rez]] that she has to keep it chill about [[The Shadowfell]] and explains how poorly [[Malachi]] took her stories about [[Kaycee Teapetal|Rez's Mom]]. [[Rez]] explains that she was trying to make him feel better, but figures out why that wouldn't work. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] agrees to give her a nod when something isn't landing right. [[Rez]] continues her effort to understand [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] and asks her many more questions. 

[[Freya]], [[Aisha]], and [[Malachi]] take their watch. [[Freya]] tries to provide context on the group: our sex lives, how we interrogate people, how she wayfinds, and then they plan our route. 

The next day we make it to about 20 minutes outside the [[Bogwood Outpost]] by early afternoon.
