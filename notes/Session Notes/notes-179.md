# Session 179

We leave the ruined temple of [[Pelor]], and head back into the central area of the village's remnants. [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] discusses if a locate object or creature is best suited, but decide to try to search for the [[Cenacle Oak Box]]. 

[[Malachi]]'s next building begins calling to him, the large nondescript stone structure on the opposite of the square. Pleading cries also come from the water around the statue. [[Hugh|Finn]] however, sees silver strands connecting to the burned wooden building and the farm houses. 

We begin with [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] seeing if she can sense the box. It seems to fade in and out of her sense, but primarily seems to read something between the stone building and the burned wooden building. She's got the sense that it's not on this plane, it's both close and not here. [[The Shadowfell]]? We discuss whether or not it's partially hidden or if we actually have to look for a way into that plane. 

After some chat, we decide to follow [[TrueSilver]]'s lead and head to the burnt wooden house. [[Malachi]] shares that while he's had interactions with [[The Shadowfell]] before, this call is different than anything he's felt before. But the shadows are clearly reacting to him. 

The wooden building was once a tavern, and [[Hugh|Finn]] can barely make out some ghostly music. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] casts divine sense and can tell the desecration permeating the whole room is condensed on the harp. A burnt and mummified corpse is still clinging to it. We come up with a classically harebrained plan to have [[Costi]] and [[Rez]] grab the harp with mage hand to try and bring it out, but the second it's touched the doors swing shut and the instruments all come to life, with ghostly musicians playing a lively jig. 

Despite a scene of laughter and music, it transitions to another scene of human sacrifice, with a bar against the door trapping the tavern guests in the burning bar. 

As the flames rise, [[Rez]], [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], [[Costi]], and [[Freya]], all are overwhelmed by the compulsion to dance. A skeletal rogue is very skilled with his bloody knife but after a quick dispel on the harp ensnaring our minds, we're able to clear them out. We spend some time trying to figure out what happened here. 

[[Costi]] finds a hidey-hole with two more burnt corpses inside. [[Hugh|Finn]] feels a longing pain from the harp, he, [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], and [[Rez]] investigate. This harp is well loved, clearly designed for travel, except for the terrible state it is in now. We're able to clear the curse from it as well. Now restored, though not repaired, we still put it in a box and take it with us. 

[[Rez]] suggests we try to send to (name?), but [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] suggests a message first, saying to the spot she's felt the box: "The Cenacle sent us, we're looking for you. Can you hear us? How did you get there?" She hears back, though intense static, a cut off "help". Trying again, "where did you go, we want to help", but gets nothing back. 

[[Aisha]] looks into the water to try to see if there's a way to get into [[The Shadowfell]] from where we are. Looking in the water, she sees the dead visage of her mother and doesn't see much else. 

[[Rez]] sends to [[Dolrak]], "The [[Cenacle]] sent us, we can help you! Are you in [[The Shadowfell]]? Where are you? What keeps you? How did you travel? Tracking the box.", he responds, "They have the [[Needles]]. Don’t try to help, this town is overrun with madness and pain. If they get you, they’ll use you too. Run."

[[Malachi]] worries this references the [[Loombrace Cenacle]] rather than referring to the town. We learn he was the target of [[F’rocidi]]'s interest, much like [[Freya]] was to [[Rawley]]. [[Costi]] suggests we try to see what we can sense about [[The Shadowfell]] from here, using the [[Shadowfell Shard]] as reference, [[Rez]] watches a tendril of smoke seep from the area right above his heart. [[Malachi]] pushes her away, calling it a wound. 

[[Samuel]] helps [[Costi]] try to investigate the area but doesn't turn up much. 

[[Hugh|Finn]] divines from [[TrueSilver]], asking, "Were the silver lines you showed a way towards our goal here or was that something you’d like us to destroy for the betterment of the world at large?" He builds a quick fire, which begins as thick black flames, ash billowing, before burning clean. The area around the fire begins to lose th decay and blood clear, above the flame, the harp forms from the smoke, slowly turning the smoke a clearer color. As the flames die, the embers reveal dozens, if not hundreds, of dark shapes in an interesting pattern on the ground. One at the bottom, glows a brief bright silver, before it fades. 

[[Aisha]] recognizes that the pattern overlays with the village and [[Bogwood Outpost]]. [[Costi]] analyzes the [[Pelor scepter]], and while it's not magical, he can sense it is important. In the right hands, it can do something. 

After a somewhat tense conversation, with [[Malachi]] raising some potential concerns with the trustworthiness of [[Loombrace Cenacle]], much to [[Freya]]'s concern and defensiveness. We decide to ask some further questions of [[Dolrak]]. 

[[Rez]] sends, "We'll help you!! Who has the needles? Are "they" in the Outpost? Has the box been opened? How are you being used? Answer in order." He replaces, "The [[Everpresent]], box is closed for now. Don’t know who is still in Outpost. They ripped me open to rip it open and went through."

We decide to ask to [[Rawley]], "Spoke with [[Dolrak]], he is with the enemy - maybe [[The Shadowfell]]. Prioritize needles, box, or him?? Box closed, for now. Needles maybe taken, were in box?" he replies, "Needles in box, if closed are safe, save [[Dolrak]] if possible if you can't, don't let him suffer. Getting [[Weaver's Needles]], is paramount." 

[[Costi]] sends [[Samuel]] to go and check out the surrounding area. At the stone structure next door, it's an administrative building. The farm houses look strangely untouched, behind the house 7 coffins are laid out. The fountain at the center of town has many coins in the water. 

After some internal debate, we decide that since both the [[Pelor scepter]] and harp have the same weighty feeling, we follow [[TrueSilver]]'s silver line and go to the farm houses, walking between them to were the coffins are. There's the sound of a woman wailing in heartbreaking grief. The coffins are all surname [[Skemf]] in the same year, 2871. We see a mound of dirt ahead, and after [[Aisha]]'s little robot takes the topsoil off. She has a jade clip falling into a hole in her skull. 

She shrieks as she disappears, her wail hits [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], knocking her unconscious. [[Hugh|Finn]] brings her back up, then cleanses the jade hair clip and pockets it. 

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] quickly looks through the homes too, and it's clear that they'd been kept in the exact same state her family left them.
