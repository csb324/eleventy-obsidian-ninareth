# Session 200

We look upon the [[Conch Wall]] that surrounds [[Nervart]], which we know are sisters to the [[Drift Wall]] in [[Brask]] and the [[Sand Wall]] around [[Freeport]]. Great news!! We're in town for the 73rd semiannual berry festival. 

Despite [[Freya]]'s extreme excitement to join the pie eating contest, we agree to go check it with the [[Wayfinders]] first and foremost. [[Molly]] disappears and [[Rez]] goes to find him while the rest go to get the stamp.

We meet [[Dunc]] a half-orc monk/fighter who Freya asks why he doesn't have knives so he goes dunc-wild on her. We learn the bridges have lots of wildlife and rumors even of metallic dragons, green dragons. We get the book stamped by [[Kaeli]], an older gnomish women, the elder of the outpost. She knows [[Rawley]] vaguely--[[Freya]] can't read her. Her deputy (we didn't get his name) is at the convention.

We try jams. There are 417 but she has 10 to try. Apparently there are ~300 "important" people who are the judges in this city of 80k. One of the jams is "M"-berry. MORTHOS. BARRY. FUCK.

[[Molly]] is looking for interesting smells. He follows a "cold"/"frosty" smell into more seedy part of town and ends up at a bar called [[Peggy's Peg]] ( ͡° ͜ʖ ͡°). [[Molly]] slips into the back door ( ͡° ͜ʖ ͡°). It's neat, clean, and well-lubricated ( ͡° ͜ʖ ͡°). [[Rez]] follows him. He finds [[Rhynilex Svenik|Rhynilex]] and [[Jade]] and sits next to them; [[Whats up with Jade's Glasses]] they flash. [[Jade]] is really "Jade" about it. Jade asks if [[Finn]] was taken by the "Big Metal Men"--the real villain that are collecting and subsuming warforged into a gigantic . Vampires are a front for it.

[[Jade]] and [[Molly]] believe each other to be interesting and ask each other questions. [[Rhynilex Svenik|Rhyn]] is about done with this and is ready to see the rest of [[The Unforged Few]]. [[Rez]] brings them to the pie eating contest.

[[Rhynilex Svenik|Rhyn]] and [[Jade]] have spoken to [[No-Karo]] who gave them a task to light a lantern in a lighthouse off the coast to "help [[Pelor]] see". After they released him from hags who had taken him, one escaped to [[the Feywild]]. They didn't see any [[Beauties of Shadow Dog Tag]], or hear the name [[Auntie Darkna]] or [[The Beauties of Shadow]]. [[Rez]] goes to take the [[Bag of Screams]] out, but it's missing! This attack happened about two weeks ago, right when we last saw it. Spooky. They show us a vial of victim's tears, a [[Vial of Endless Woe]] which allows us to experience the worst moment of someone's life: [[Freya]] wakes from a deep sleep with their legs missing, and the grief of never running or walking again. [[Costi]] smells smoke returning home from a night out, rounding the corner just as his house falls to the fire. [[Rez]] reads a letter informing her that after dozens of magical medical clinics, she's politely informed they will not be able to help her - sealing her death. [[Jade]] takes one every day to learn their secrets, sharing [[The Curator]] is interested but she is not forced to do it. 

[[No-Karo]] was taken pirates, got kidnapped, so [[Rhynilex Svenik|Rhyn]] and [[Jade]] then killed a bunch of them before crashing their ship. They didn't see any cool weapons or strange stones though. 

[[Rhynilex Svenik|Rhyn]] and [[Costi]] have a moment, discussing that seeing other people's traumas can make your own have new perspective, though not many are as bad as killing your own father. She gives [[Costi]] an appreciate once over too. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] asks if [[Rhynilex Svenik|Rhynilex]] and [[Jade]] have learned anything more about [[Morthos]], [[Rhynilex Svenik|Rhyn]] confirms that lighting the lantern should maybe illuminate some new information. 

[[Freya]] and [[Rez]] bet who can eat more pies, naming terms. If [[Rez]] wins, [[Freya]] has to talk about how cool she is during her [[Wayfinders]] speech. If [[Freya]] wins, [[Rez]] has to wear her copper dress and flash [[Gimzid]]. [[Jade]] enters too. 

[[Freya]] makes it through 17 pies by the end of the 10 minutes, but in the midst of her glory girl bosses too close to the sun and voms. Because it's ridiculous, [[Rez]] does unusually well. She eats 26 total pies in 20 minutes. When the crust clears, [[Rez]] is the winner! She gets a sash as the "berry festivals number one eater", a crown that looks like pie crust, seemingly made of gold, and 300 gold. She gets a stay tonight at the Bakers Suite as well. 

During the excitement [[Molly]] steals a fine ruby cut in the shape of a berry, as well as a spiky yellow berry he's never seen before.
