# Session 176

[[Rez]] pulls all the siblings together to sheepishly give the update on what happened at [[Pleetos Alentas estate]].  

[[Freya]] makes a quick plug on the way for [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] to call her as more than just a dagger, at least a battle ax. 

We decide to dig into to some of the intel from the letter. [[Eirian|E]] is going to try learn more about the [[Copper Cove]] and [[Grandor's Queen]]. [[Zmaj]] copies the letter to learn more about the siphoning. We give [[Vask]] some lead boxes and the black mail material to [[Eirian]]. [[Zmaj]] is going to work to catalogue them. 

[[Zmaj]] thinks it's best that we split up again, that we should not all be together. [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] got to [[Quaath]], [[Vask]] will leave with the [[The Silver Halves]] and not stay in the [[The Silver Tongue]]. 

[[Zmaj]] and [[Rez]] have a short chat, [[Rez]] a little more candid about her anger at herself, and [[Zmaj]] is more understanding than she might've expected, saying we all slip up. However, the [[Unscaled]] having [[Rez]]'s blood makes her a liability - to herself and to [[Rez's Family]], so she needs to stop working on this. [[Rez]] reluctantly agrees, not wanting to put her family at risk. In more self-awareness than typical, she apologizes and accepts that this is the result of her slip up. They agree that this could be more dangerous and serious than they can tackle right now. 

[[Freya]] tries to convince [[Rawley]] to meet tonight, and he says no. She's tired of waiting. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] works on the harp. [[Costi]] suggests a fun evening with [[Rez]], they try to get a prickly guard to arrest them for doing absolutely nothing illegal. They leave a bottle of wine as a thank you. 

The next morning, [[Rawley]] heads to the hotel, tells [[Freya]] to go and get [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]], we have to make some stops and move to a new location. He heads to a bar, [[The First Leg]] to introduce us to a drow and a tiefling. 

The tiefling is small, 5ft tall, maroon coloring, gold eyes, dark brown hair braided out of her face. Practical but well made clothing - named [[Aisha]]. Near her is a drow, 6'1" lean build with floppy silver hair, grayish purple skin, and deep burgundy eyes - he's very pretty, despite a broken nose and a healed poorly pinky finger, [[Malachi]]. 

[[Rawley]] moves them, and us, to a reserved backroom. He talks to the bartender, and we all get the spell seeming on us to hid our appearance. He's going to take us to the [[Dovecrest Silo]] to meet with the [[Loombrace Cenacle]]. 

We manage to each create an unassuming affect as we split into groups to walk across this city and keep attention off of us. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] slips a gold coin to a talented young busker. We pass by [[The Copper Opera]], a flimsy large theatre building, painted copper with a super garish marquee. It's dead. [[Rez]] sighs.

Eventually we reach a large silo with a white dove on top. Through the doors there's an elevator, after a long ride, the top is warded and 'safe'. [[Naya]] and [[Akros]] will be here soon, they mention [[Rawley]] is [[Freya]]'s sponsor (though apparently [[Akros]] and [[Naya]] are sharing), and [[F’rocidi]] is theirs. 

We learn that [[Aisha]] sought out joining the [[Wayfinders]], and [[Malachi]] used to be in the navy. They're from [[Durmask]] but then went to [[K'falgaard|Kfalgaard]]. 

[[F'rocidi]] is a short half dwarf half tiefling. Two more members of the the [[Cenacle]] are coming. One is a dark skinned human man, shaved head, dressed in yellow flowing robes in the [[Tinkate]] style named [[Schelva]], and a halfling dressed in green traveling clothes, named [[Oyin Ifernell]]. 

[[Rawley]] shares they are a small group within the [[Wayfinders]], the [[Loombrace Cenacle]] has been around for a long time. Most [[Wayfinders]] don't notice their internal divides, but early on in their founding realized they could do broader political goals, which created a number of splinter groups and secret societies. They want to stop the [[Everpresent]], but when we ask from doing what, they say there's a bit of a delay - we have to be 'bound to the [[Cenacle]]'. 

They were potentially going to induct a fourth, [[Dolrak]], who was carrying something important for that but has gone missing. It would've taken him by the [[Bogwood Outpost]] in the [[Narathian Plains]]. He says there's some places in the [[Planes of existence]] where great tragedies can create holes to [[The Shadowfell]]. It was part of the [[Millik]] led efforts on the bugbears, and was burned several times by dragonfire. 

We need to find the [[Cenacle Oak Box]]. They've all voted and accepted [[Freya]], [[Malachi]], and [[Aisha]] for induction, and the rest of [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] will come along. [[Rawley]] says there's the binding agent that will provide us some magical protection, but won't tell us what it is, but will know if it's been opened. 

At [[Malachi]]'s urging, [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] messages [[F'rocidi]] to encourage him to give us some more info. We learn it's time sensitive, we must be 'bound' by the start of the [[Wayfinder Annual Gathering]].

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] does what we do best, we push. We shock them with some of our knowledge, tease more, but they don't cave. [[Malachi]] tries to call out [[Rawley]]'s tactic of keeping information dammed, has started to fail. They still don't cave, and hope we will bond. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] threatens that if this process is a formality, we will not be so cooperative in the future. [[Rawley]] reassures us that it is magically necessary. [[Oyin Ifernell]] gives [[Hugh|Finn]] a detailed description of the [[Cenacle Oak Box]]. 

We decide to head to the [[The Silver Tongue]], but it's warned that we must keep up the act, and that [[Freya]] as well as [[Malachi]] are probably being watched. 

[[Rez]] sendings to [[Zmaj]], "Hiiii. Things are moving on operation undead! Operation no undead? Anyway. Are [[Gia'Thoren]] in town? Can we tree to the [[Bogwood Outpost]]? Or the plains?" he responds, "Close, yes."
