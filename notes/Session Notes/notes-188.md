# Session 188

[[Malachi]] gives her a description of her to help [[Hugh|Finn]] scry on her. It doesn't work. 

We arrive at the Brass bar, the [[The Silver Halves]] are being themselves in all the various ways you would expect. 

[[Rez]] introduces [[Malachi]] and [[Aisha]] to [[Vask]], who is showboaty and very pleased to meet them. Immediately flirts, and then is beyond thrilled when [[Costi]] shares that [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] has been quite lingeringly ill from his poisoning. [[Vask]] teases [[Rez]] about [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], which she's awkward about. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] and [[Hugh|Finn]] go to warm up [[Victoria Several|Victoria]] and [[Magister]]. They learn [[The Silver Halves]] tried to visit us while we were in [[The Shadowfell]] longer than expected. [[Malachi]] introduces himself and tries to pitch them hunting down [[Uhlara]], though it doesn't land immediately.

Their current job is going into [[Yent Lake]], to seek a wreck of a shipping magnate. A woman named [[Tin Tin]] hired them to retrieve a manifest. [[Victoria Several|Victoria]] agrees that [[Malachi]] can join this trip, take his pay as contribution towards the job afterwards. Anything valuable [[Uhlara]] has will be [[The Silver Halves]], and he can keep working off the rest unless he's dead weight, and if so he'll have a few months before interest kicks in. [[Rez]] warns them not to let in a kraken. They don't think the risk is high. 

They negotiate for a minute. [[Malachi]] agrees, and offers to buy a round for [[The Silver Halves]] to break the ice. [[Vask]] goes with him and tries to tell [[Malachi]] the way to [[Magister]]s heart is through pranks, but can barely hold back the laughter. [[Malachi]] turns it back around on [[Aisha]]. 

After a brief catch up, we dive into our "need a terrorist" pickle, but as soon as we put up the lantern we do see the scrying mote. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] tries to pivot on the reason being for this that we're worried [[Colvia Ghul]] will attack us. [[Victoria Several|Victoria]] dives in too. [[Rez]] sends to [[Zmaj]], "hey, are you scrying on us, like, RIGHT now? No reason", he responds, "…no. Are you getting scryed on right now? Don’t respond to this in case you need to fight."

[[Victoria Several|Victoria]] agrees, if they get back in time, [[Vask]] can help blow up some stuff. His quick response lets [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] intuit that he's still chasing after her, but hasn't yet caught the car. 

[[Malachi]] ropes [[Freya]] into a prank to steal one of [[Magister]]s quills and plant it on [[Vask]] as pay back for [[Vask]] trying to set him up. It works. [[Vask]] promises to get him back, and also maybe get him back? They're flirting, it's hard to know. 

[[Hugh|Finn]] pulls [[Magister]] aside and tells him to pay closer attention to [[Aisha]], calling her an academic. [[Magister]] recognizes he maybe was too tough on her, he didn't appreciate her insinuation he needed to be 'worked on'. [[Hugh|Finn]] also shares that he's been struggling with the pressure of keeping everyone alive. He's had to bring back one of the [[The Unforged Few]] three times, and he's not sure he can keep going. [[Magister]] says it's why many who heal retire early - plus he should spend more time around those that are immortal. [[Hugh|Finn]] asks if [[Magister]] thinks [[Vask]] will sleep with [[Malachi]], [[Magister]] shares that he thinks [[Vask]] likes to have the option, but doesn't often seem to follow through. He's obviously nursing a torch for [[Victoria Several|Victoria]] and is maybe trying to prove he's serious, in a way that goes against the nature of [[Rez's Family]]. [[Hugh|Finn]] agrees that he knows from traveling with [[Rez]]. Asking about [[The Self Made Men]], [[Daemon]] is on a cruise off the coast, [[Yellen Ziv]] is working in mining efforts in [[Zilvengrod]].

[[Magister]] finds [[Aisha]], apologizing. They find some common ground in the drive of academics. He shares there is room on the submersible, if she travels with [[Malachi]]. [[Magister]] it also looks at her firearm. It seems to respond to dunamancy, supercharging it when a catalyst is cast on it beforehand, creating a fourth barrel. [[Magister]] offers to compare notes. She agrees to join (sorry about ya [[Colvia Ghul]]).

When we return to our rooms, [[Aisha]] finds a gift from [[Colvia Ghul]] waiting for her, it's a rare first edition book, a combination of advice of artificery from a collection of rare experts, including a forge master from [[Plane of Fire]] and an expert from [[Dis]]. [[Colvia Ghul]] shares some annotations for her on particular parts of her thesis. [[Malachi]] and [[Aisha]] discuss what she should do. [[Aisha]] worries that it's charity from [[Magister]] and she wouldn't give back to [[The Silver Halves]], but with [[Colvia Ghul]] she's not naive and knows she wants something out of this but thinks she could gain benefit and find out what she wants. 

[[Aisha]] and [[Malachi]] come to tell us the plan and say their goodbyes. It doesn't end on a sweet note, [[Aisha]] convinces most of [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] that she would never intentionally share information about us, but we're all more concerned about [[Colvia Ghul]]'s ability to learn more, about us, or even [[Aisha]]'s own priorities. Trying to flag that [[Aisha]] is underestimating her, and it especially strains with [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] who warns that thinking you can outmaneuver someone is the most dangerous position to be in. 

Before bed, [[Freya]] checks the vial of blood she was gifted from [[The Curator]]. It now matches the changes to her skin, with dark red and purple striations, and a strong odor of [[The Shadowfell]]. She asks [[The Curator]] if she needs to do anything with it, and is told no - just keep it. Maybe she'll need it later. 

In the morning, as [[Malachi]] prepares to leave, [[Rez]] hands him personalized prank material for [[Vask]], along with a warning to stop himself if he gets the urge to say anything crazy to him, no more detail needed. [[Malachi]] has a precious goodbye with [[Hugh|Finn]], calling him a kind of gentle he didn't know he deserved - [[Hugh|Finn]] argues those have been some of the most important people in his life, and he didn't deserve it, but [[Malachi]] does. 

 [[Costi]] gives [[Aisha]] a sweet goodbye, apologizing for being so cold the night before. He explains a bit about the powerlessness he felt as a taste with [[Julian Slater-Runehorn|Julian]], and how it's significantly worse from [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]'s perspective, which helps to explain her reaction. He warns that [[Colvia Ghul]] seems similar to [[Julian Slater-Runehorn|Julian]], and some decks are stacked against you before you can even play. At the same time, how do you ever beat them without trying? [[Aisha]] thanks him and hopes she can prove it wrong, [[Costi]] says if anyone can do it, it's her. They share an awkward hug. She hides in his pocket a letter, and enchanted sending parchment, that allow 20 uses to send messages to each other.

[[Malachi]] and [[Aisha]] leave the [[The Unforged Few]].
