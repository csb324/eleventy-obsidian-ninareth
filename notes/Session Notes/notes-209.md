# Session 209

We plan to have [[Molly]] stakeout [[The Seven Copper Opera]] before we go to find where the basement entrance is or guard rotations etc. [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] talks backward to be sure we get enough sleep. [[Freya]] is tasked to party publicly for cover story during this time. The rest of us will be hanging at the Salty Wet. 

[[Freya]] buys drinks for so many [[Wayfinders]], gets blitzed for an hour, then leads a parade bar crawling to the [[Songside]]. 

[[Molly]] unfortunately has a hard time seeing anything. Through lots of interesting and deeply confusing conversations we learn that we have to put him back on the right track. [[Samuel]] helps. Oops, he's TOO well hidden, he's in a barrel. 

As [[Freya]] shakes off the partying crowd, [[Molly]] spots someone carrying a large crate, who does some potentially intentional steps, and then walks through a wall. [[Samuel]] goes to look and see if he can find stones to activate - though it looks like maybe it's got both more on the stones and some possible somatic components as well. The dark skinned half-elf comes back out 25 minutes later without the box. 

[[Molly]] tails the guy, we slip out of the bar to surreptitiously put on our disguise hats, and plan to make an alley [[My Hole]] to interrogate him in. It's cartoonish but we get him in, tied up and seal it to talk to him. 

After some unintentional harassment, we threaten him into telling us what he delivered to the basement at the [[The Seven Copper Opera]]. He says the manifest showed 3 rust monsters. He's been delivering creatures there for about 8 years, for either storage at [[The Rumble]] or for plays. We give some vagueness about the threat to the world, but he believes us. He delivered a displacer beast 7 months ago, and security got much more intense 3 years ago. He says security checks your paperwork and someone else takes the monsters deeper. He's got a heart of gold turns out, isn't the sharpest, but agrees to help if there's a threat to the world. He's [[Kelvin]]. His wife [[Jinni]] expecting their first child. We plan to hide our own evidence with a modify memory and then after he helps us get in send him to [[Henrik]] for a chance of protection. 

He helps us come up with a plan to save him and his family. We come up with a free trip they've won, romantically, along with their brother in law, to stay in their room, order room service so they won't starve. The Secret Copper Sweepstakes. [[Rez]] will tell [[Eirian]] later. We plan to get them safely to [[Arkhosia (City)]] after everything. [[Molly]] will sneak behind him with [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] in [[My Hole]]. [[Rez]] modifies his memory. He is so wholesomely happy. 

[[Kelvin]] leads us in, with a hidden [[Molly]] successfully following him. [[Kelvin]] is able to convince them to take him down to fix the box and bring a number of guards with him. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] does a locate object on an [[Freya's Mysterious Circles|Eye of the Everpresent]]. Turns out that requires a wisdom save for some unknown reason. It hurts her and she doesn't learn any locations - but we learn connecting the weave to one is a bad idea generally. [[Molly]] tries to smell the smelly smell from the [[Equinox Citadel]] here. He finds it in a storage room for [[Roland's Last Charge]] set to perform on the day of partial [[synchronicity]]. 

The room inside is dark, with set storage, including tapestries and costumes. There's a massive blue dragon head. We code name: [[Freya]] - [[Crusher]], [[Molly]] - [[Squawk]], [[Costi]] - [[And]], [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] - [[Mumford]], [[Rez]] - [[Sonz]]. 

[[Sonz]], [[Crusher]], and [[Squawk]] go to investigate the dragonhead. [[Crusher]] does a fly by detect magic, it doesn't show a school but it does glimmer? They're not real dragon scales according to [[Sonz]], but it has maybe a coating of ground scale powder. [[Squawk]] dives in and it's lined with a cushion to land on. 

[[And]] finds what seems like a lead covering on the floor, [[Crusher]] comes to help lift it out. Below it is a trapdoor and has both abjuration and evocation on it. 

[[Mumford]] casts silence on both sides of the door, [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] is 65ft away, tied on by [[And]] and [[Sonz]] with mage hand. [[Sonz]] makes a metal shield and then plans to roll it to barricade the door. Oops it's trapped, and can't be disarmed without triggering the magical trap. [[Squawk]] dodges as it shoots out 7 beams.

Once opened, we see it leads to stairs downwards, as soon as we land in a nondescript room, the stairs behind us vanish. There's four stones, one in each wall (blue, green, red, yellow). [[Costi]] and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] are paralyzed in expressions of agony. [[Molly]] sees that the walls have a faint inscription on the walls, but can't read it, and asks for help. [[Rez]] reads it as a quote from [[Roland's Last Charge]] script, noticing where it begins. Some words are etched more deeply, all the ones dealing with love, passion, or romance. Kissing [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] sadly doesn't fix it, at least right away. She and [[Costi]] are both able to break away from the immediate effects in the room. 

[[Rez]] remembers in the play [[Roland the Bold]] is named [[Lemla]], who always wears blue. All the stones can move as well as be depressed. [[Costi]] kisses the blue sapphire stone. It fucking works. There's a door on the other side, [[Molly]] can make out a faint sound but can't tell what it is. 

In the room there's a door on the opposite side of the office. The sound is coming from beyond it. There's a painting we recognize from the [[Necromantic Underground Chapel]] - of [[Morthos]]. Above it is an [[Freya's Mysterious Circles|Eye of the Everpresent]]. There's some jars on the desk, with a raven's quill, and a bar cart. 

[[Costi]] and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] look in the desk. There's paper periodically set to reduce itself to ash. There's lots of correspondence, some of it directly with [[His Eminence]]. There's also a folded piece of paper that can be folded to swap different letters, it's a key to a code. 

[[Molly]] looks at the jars, two have pinky fingers, one an index fingers, and one set of index and point fingers. [[Rez]] and [[Molly]] scoops the jars of fingers into [[My Hole]]. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] sees that every single item in the room has abjuration magic on it. And we've already triggered an alarm. 

We feel like we have to investigate the door, but we still don't recognize the language, we hear hammers though. We're all around [[My Hole]] to gtfo. [[Costi]] sends an eye through the door and sees a rift in reality, some sort of rend. There's about 20 creatures - some slaad, many in construction outfits, building a nonsensical building. There's also [[gith'zarei]] directing the work. The portal is directly to the [[Plane of Limbo]]. [[Rez]] and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] try to plant that [[Alvar]] from [[Below the Wall]] sent us here. Who knows if anyone is listening. 

[[Molly]] gets [[The Weaver]]'s shroud, the whistle, and carries all of us out. It miraculously works.

[[Freya]] immediately sends through her link to [[Rawley]], "fuck fuck fuck there's so much to tell you, it's fine. Do you have a tuning fork to [[Plane of Limbo]]?" We say we need to talk to him right now. He asks if we saw anything to [[His Majesty]] but we say no. 

We plan to be seen in a bar for a few moments, pop by the citadel for a quick debrief and then get to rest so [[Freya]] can sleep and still meet [[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]].
