# Session 173

We arrive to the site [[Pik]] and [[Pak]] held, and their solo hostage. [[Eirian|E]] and [[Vask]] head back to the city, leaving the interrogation to [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]], [[Zmaj]], and [[Zalvaris|Zalv]]. 

Our prisoner is a human, named [[Cole]] in his mid-twenties. He doesn't have any visible draconic modifications, and seems healthy enough. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] sets up "truth telling ritual", [[Freya]] joins in on the intimidation. [[Rez]] lyingly pitches having him be his mole. He's clearly terrified.

He joined recently, about 4 months, after his older sister [[Vrekka]] had been involved for the last few years. He claims he wanted consistent money and got decent at fighting before signing up. 

When he first signed up, [[Pleetos Alentas]] was in charge, before [[Nebrur Sunbreaker]] took over. Served mostly on guard duty, claims not to know what was happened downstairs, though when pressed admits there were rumors. 

He says shipments came infrequently, at least once a month. There were passcodes and passwords, though he never learned them. The last about two week ago. Says there were lots of wards, so "the rest of them", will definitely know what happened here. 

He believes this location was 25-30% of the full group. He thinks 35-40 were there today, we killed 34, so at least a few got away. There are two other hideouts, some talked about how they went to [[Taleem]] and [[Mulfara]]. 

[[Rez]] asked how they identified each other, to know they belonged, after small talk they mentioned "balanced/ing the scales". 

[[Cole]] says [[Vrekka]] was dating (or married to?) a merchant who was doing some runs out in the [[Turian Waste]], and when people went out to find him they were attacked by [[blue dragons]]. She never got over it, and was found by people at a bar in the [[Frontier]]. When he dances around why they would scout her out, he alludes to some hating, but others wanting to "level the playing field". 

He says definitely it was discussed that participation from the [[Order of the Platinum Dragon]], specifically a "silver scale" ([[Silver Scale Jazat]] who [[Vask]] threatened? Another?), but it was especially hush hush. 

[[Cole]] says they call themselves the [[Unscaled]]. [[Rez]] asks how, after all of this, he feels about dragons and at his weak "praise [[Bahamut]]" [[Rez]] has had enough, and [[Zmaj]] knocks him out. [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] walks off with him. 

[[Rez]] pitches all of [[Rez's Family]] who's in town to just go put some pressure on the [[Thelran Asari]], [[Zmaj]] councils patience and waiting for the traitor silver scale to make a mistake. [[Rez]] begrudgingly agrees. She does push for at least a few of them to vet the current head of the [[Platinum Sanctuary of Thelran Asari]], who despite being a gold scale, could be a part of the network of traitors working with this particular silver scale. [[Zmaj]] agrees. They plan to have [[Hugh|Finn]] have zone of truth. They also plan to inspect the key from [[Pleetos Alentas]], before scrying it. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] starts to read through the [[Unscaled]]'s notes, [[Rez]] goes to blow off steam by gambling recklessly with [[Vask]]. [[Freya]] tries to help con people but does NOT make it easier, [[Rez]] and [[Vask]] do a decent enough job anyway. They discuss how to be sure [[Zalvaris|Zalv]]'s torture of [[Cole]] doesn't extend tooo too far, [[Vask]] plans to rile [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] up enough tomorrow he should be angry enough to cut things off. They talk briefly about how to handle the [[Order of the Platinum Dragon]], agree on the need to strong arm them but hold off for now. 

[[Freya]] and [[Hugh|Finn]] go to walk through the [[Equinox Citadel]], every day it seems like more [[Wayfinders]] are arriving. Out of the 8,000 total, only about 500 are here already. [[Freya]] is greeted by [[Lirnis Kilzire]], who they introduce to [[Hugh|Finn]]. He reminds [[Freya]] of the exhibitions. He also pushes [[Freya]] to bring [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] around. They pick up though that he was nervous at not knowing [[Freya]] was there, and he rushed to meet her. He was also tracking where [[Freya]] was staying, which he says is for all the [[Wayfinders]], and sent someone there to check on her but noticed she moved somewhere else. They head to a reading group on moss growth patterns. [[Hugh|Finn]] impresses them with his interest, and [[Lirnis Kilzire]] gives him one of his personal visitor passes, plus a credit on the paper regarding moss! 

[[Zmaj]] comes to visit [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] while she's alone, offers to fix [[Costi]], taking the money out of his pocket, but [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] pays for the remainder of the funds. He tells her they've sent about 200 soldiers to the area of the [[Unscaled Hideout]] as well as a few more skilled soldiers inside. 

[[Rez]] goes to ask [[Zmaj]] for strategic advice, but it turns out into more of a heart to heart that the [[Unscaled]] have gotten under his scales - he's worried they've been ahead of us every time, and have taken out two of [[Rez's Family]]. [[Zmaj]] is haunted by the idea that they'll take more of us, or that he'll underestimate them to our detriment, or that they planned for the [[Unscaled Hideout]] to be burned, [[Pleetos Alentas]] wasn't there and what if that was intentional? [[Rez]] reminds him that they are all working together, and their siblings are still in the [[Weird Boulder]] so there must be hope. Plus, they've already made so much progress after only knowing about this organization for a mere few months. They share some learnings about being around the lesser races, who aren't quite as lesser as they previously assumed. Turns out there's quite a bit of life mortals pack into their limited years. [[Zmaj]] offers a few more of their siblings to get involved, they decide to wait on getting [[Li]] into things. 

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] plans to push our outreach in the [[Plaza of the Faithful]]. 

Oops, [[Freya]] reveals she still has [[Sali]], and more over, she agreed to be a part of [[Zalvaris|Zalv]]'s hoard. [[Rez]] storms off, the rest of [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] tries to convinces [[Freya]] that people aren't objects to be claimed. [[Freya]] didn't dislike it. When [[Costi]] argues that there's more dangerous pacts she's been a part of, [[The Curator]] pings no. It stumps her. [[Hugh|Finn]] brings up how being property is actually deeply serious, and [[Freya]] is not appreciating the severity. 

[[Rez]] goes to yell at [[Zalvaris|Zalv]], but instead finds him sobbing. Instead she asks about how much the attack on the [[Unscaled Hideout]] makes him feel better, he says for an hour. He thinks combining the drinking with the vengeance might make the relief last longer - [[Rez]] tries to ground him by sharing her worries for [[Zmaj]], and then offering connect him to [[Lora]]'s descendant, [[Ravona Silentreed]]. They agree to share [[Freya]], which means sharing a bit of hoard with [[Vask]] as well. 

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] discusses [[Freya]] being in a hoard, which inevitably snowballs into [[Rez]] admitting she considers [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] part of her hoard. It doesn't land the way she thought it would. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], [[Costi]] and [[Hugh|Finn]] are worried about subjugation or being owned, [[Rez]] is confused - she says it's important and an honor to be in a dragon's hoard. It doesn't seem to be received as such. They agree to sleep on it.
