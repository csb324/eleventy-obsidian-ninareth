# Session 189

We discuss how to frame a terroristic threat, brainstorming on the [[Fire Raisers]], potentially having [[Costi]] impersonate [[Niko]], and maybe trying to also get [[Captain Fretze|Fretze]] off his trail. We discuss the politics of the [[Wayfinders]], [[Thelran Asari]] leadership, and [[Fire Raisers]] goals. 

[[Hugh|Finn]] tells us he's going to see [[Eva Sidorev|Eva]], we encourage him to sending her and get specifics. Try to ask what support he needs, but he's flustered and unsure. It's been 60 years. [[Hugh|Finn]] sends, "Hello! What time today? Is today still good? It doesn’t have to be today but I’d like it to be", she replies, "Sure! How about lunch, 1:00?" They decide we should go see [[Costi's Dad|Vladimir Grosu]], which is nearby, while they lunch.

[[Costi]] wants to sell the pre-sundering gold coins at the [[Teapetal Emporium]], and [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] tags along, but [[Freya]], [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], and [[Hugh|Finn]] go to the bar. We work with [[Jenna]] again, who offers us 300 gp for them. We give that right back for a number of healing potions. 3,900 - the steep discount comes with the promise that we offer first right of refusal for everything we sell in [[Thelran Asari]]. 

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] reconvenes with a wild and all over the place conversation about who has exes and what that even means. After a chat that does not help his anxiety at all, we split. 

[[Costi]] and [[Rez]] go to talk with [[Eirian|E]]. [[Eirian]] wasn't with [[Samson]] at the [[Thelran Asari Council]], [[Costi]] warns her about the overlap with [[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]], [[Synchrona]], and the day of partial [[synchronicity]] at the end of the [[Wayfinder Annual Gathering]]. From the not perfect memory of [[Samson]], it doesn't seem like [[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]] was particularly vocal in the meetings. [[Costi]] asks if she can help [[Nadia]] get into the [[Niraani Sisterhood]]. [[Eirian]] agrees, but wants to evaluate her first. We'll bring her later, after 3. [[Costi]] asks about the political revolution, she recommends we just go to the tavern. A drunk [[Rez]] latches onto that idea. 

On the way there, [[Costi]] tries to entertain [[Rez]] with a game of what the fuck is up is that, immediately asking about [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]. A drunken [[Rez]] launches into a convoluted explanation, focusing mostly on the mystery of why [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] would possibly say no if it's not what she really wanted, and wouldn't say anything about it. She says there's got to be a threat, or compulsion, or enchantment if she WANTs to by CAN'T. [[Rez]] points at her bracelet of [[The Game]] and requests [[Costi]] look for any similar binding on [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]. [[Costi]] points out to [[Rez]] she was broken up with. This is news to her. He theorizes it *could be* the [[Unscaled]] trying to ruin [[Rez]]'s life (she latches on to that idea) but it's probably not. He walks [[Rez]] through [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] being often scared of the worst case outcome, and there's definitely a not-that-small part of this that is the complication of being involved with an immortal dragon. [[Rez]] doesn't fully follow, but starts to consider [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]'s emotionally worried about if things go poorly. She asks if she should remind [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] of the good and fun things? [[Costi]] isn't sure how to fix this, but suggests a [[Sanderson Brando]] novel that talks exactly about this, the [[Scaled Heart Trilogy]], he gives her the final one, [[Pierced]]. [[Rez]] is ready. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] and [[Freya]] go to hang out, and end up talking through [[Freya]]'s speech. They consider this is an intentional set up by [[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]]. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] tees her off talking about the [[Gruung]]. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] tries to lead [[Freya]] to a point through her ranting. 

[[Hugh|Finn]] goes to meet [[Eva Sidorev|Eva]], bringing flowers and a loaf of bread. After much trepidation, finally sees [[Eva Sidorev|Eva]] again after 60 years. After some small talk to open up, they both share that they didn't know what happened to the other, or if they were lost. [[Hugh|Finn]] shares that he was wiped, and at one point fully abandoned the [[Eberronian Military]]. Eventually he found himself back there, but he shares that things are different now. [[Eva Sidorev|Eva]] said she also had to leave, once they took [[Hugh|Finn]]. He's glad because it gave her a full life, and she shares it was a happy one. [[Hugh|Finn]] says hers was the face he never forgot, she saved him. He meets her husband, who is happy to give them the space to reconnect. [[Eva Sidorev|Eva]] tells him about her children, and they spend some time swapping stories about their lives. They share dinner with one of her kids and husband, he has ambitious of retiring and opening a restaurant - “A bright quiet restaurant where people can eat food”. After their meal, [[Hugh|Finn]] has a walk and talk with [[Eva Sidorev|Eva]]'s husband, who agrees they can all be friends, as long as [[Hugh|Finn]] doesn't plan on ruining their lives, though he's not too worried about that.

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] all meets up together afterwards. [[Rez]] whines to go to the tavern, [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] sobers her up. We agree to chat with [[Nadia]], bring her to talk to [[Eirian]], hang at the [[The Silver Tongue]] with [[Costi's Dad|Vladimir Grosu]], and then do the taverns in the outer part of the city tonight. 

We all go to see [[Costi's Dad|Vladdy Daddy]] and [[Nadia]]. Surprise! She gets a big fun night. But first, surpirse! She has an interview. [[Nadia]] is a bit taken aback, she didn't believe [[Costi]] would pull it off. [[Hugh|Finn]] takes her on horse back, the rest of us walk with [[Costi's Dad|Vladimir Grosu]]. [[Costi]] shares his worry about [[Hugh|Finn]] lately, his stress of fighting and keeping people alive. [[Costi's Dad|Vladdy Daddy]] agrees to talk to him, but says he's only now realizing that he barely knows the real [[Hugh|Finn]], now that he has his memories back. [[Hugh|Finn]] never mentioned [[Eva Sidorev|Eva]] to him, despite how important she was, because he didn't even remember. 

At the [[The Silver Tongue]], [[Eirian]] starts to put [[Nadia]] to the test, beginning with playing tough. After a 30 minute test, being dramatic but in a way to push her, [[Eirian]] agrees to write her a letter of recommendation, and tells her that her reputation is now tied to hers - she must work for it. [[Eirian|E]] gives [[Nadia]] or one of her flutes. After leaving, [[Eirian|E]] maintaining her act the whole time. [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] celebrates with her, and [[Costi's Dad|Vladdy Daddy]] decides to stay to talk with her. 

The rest of us head out to the taverns.
