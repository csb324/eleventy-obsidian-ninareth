# Session 172

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] is rejoined by [[Zalvaris|Zalv]], [[Zmaj]], [[Eirian]], [[Vask]], [[Ferdinand]], [[Ghez Yhutl]], [[Melnir]], and [[Samson]]. 

[[Eirian|E]] had two captives, but they didn't make it past [[Vask]]. We regret not planning to have speak with dead prepared. 

[[Freya]] asks [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] for [[Sali]] first, but then counters a long-term for her / short-term for him borrowing. She doesn't live that long comparatively. [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] is hesitant to give up part of his hoard, which [[Freya]] then offers herself as a weapon for him to have collected (somewhere [[Rez]] is objecting!!!). [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] likes the sound of that enough, that he agrees - asking her to continue the tradition of his weapons making him bleed. Agreeing, [[Freya]] plans to meet him once we're all back in [[Thelran Asari]]. 

[[Zmaj]] directs all of us in our search: the draconic abomination, the bodies, [[Bhula Orbnose]] in particular, and the room broadly for more passages. 
* [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] and [[Hugh|Finn]] search the room, there's four exits, plus the lift. Not much is in the space. The stones are very fastidiously hewn, the stone work walls all catch their attention. On one of the pillars, by the water in the center of the room, there's a faint carved line revealing a panel. Inside is machinery, a small lever. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] also notes as she walks past the areas with the purple stones, the air felt weird. A pressure almost. It reminds us of the dunamantic trap that connected us to the plane with the captive dragon. [[Costi]] can tell it's graviturgy and connected to another plane ([[There's Plainly Something Wrong with the Planes]]) - it feels like pinprick in the planes. It's dormant now but it now has a permanent mar in the [[Prime Material]]. We find and circle the spots where all three were used.
* [[Costi]] has a cocaine hangover, and [[Rez]] is super tired thanks to her extra reactions -- so they help [[Zmaj]] look at the bodies. Overall, we take 4 fine silvered daggers, 2 +1 deathbrand axes, dragon pieces: all have 1 red and 1 blue, 1 tooth, 1 dried eye, 1 silver dragon scale, and 372 GP. [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] has the barbarian's weapon, but we also find 2 [[purple stones]] - they are light and seemingly fragile, but don't seem to break unless the intention is to break. 
* [[Ghez Yhutl]] and [[Melnir]] look at the abomination, we notice that [[Melnir]] has a professional dissection kit and seems to maybe include torturing items. 
* [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] and [[Freya]] search [[Bhula Orbnose]]. They find 4 dragonscales in her clothes: 2 gold, 1 silver, 1 copper and several fragments on her body: 7 sq/in black dragon hide and 9 sq/in green dragonhide. 12 platinum. A notable ring and necklace (with one scale of every color: white black green blue red silver gold copper brass bronze and bits of draconic organs, 1 fang, and 1 claw), plus 1/3 vial dragon blood wiped from the remnants of the tongue she swallowed.  

After clearing the initial part of the main room, [[Eirian]] takes her people to do a sweep along the tunnels to be sure no one is left. 

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] pulls the mechanism to drain the water from the center of the room. Once cleared, we hear an now somewhat familiar roar. [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] takes [[Freya]] and [[Rez]]'s summoned copper to explore. At the bottom, there are two chambers that are sealed behind thick doors. It's clearly mad, and so they stab it to death. 

[[Zmaj]] sends [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] to search the bottom, [[Zmaj]] and [[Zalvaris|Zalv]] each take a tunnel. At the bottom of the water, we find a couple of doors. Through one of the doors, we see a chamber filled with poisonous odors, overwhelming anyone who tries to enter. Inside is rotting mutilated draconic parts. The floors are covered in inches of congealed flesh, 25 chained and manacled posts, some for humanoids and some for draconic forms. It's either that there is such a lack of care for the beings inside the room that it was never cleaned, or that the piles of gore are intentional. There's blood streaked parchment on the counters ([[Rez]] grabs that). We spot another lever by the door we entered into, which it seems like allowed the chains that opened the cage of the first abomination we killed. Searching the cabinets, they're suspiciously empty, and one door has burning papers inside. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] tries to message to [[Eirian|E]] "We think there were more people who were down here, freed the creature, maybe left but maybe not". She responds, "Yes that seems about right, one room we walked by is much more on fire than it was before". 

Following the hallway around, we find a similar gore room on the other side, with a robust package of notes on how to dissect dragons. There's some annotated books as well. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] takes them. 

Racing deeper into the compound, [[Hugh|Finn]] notices the smell of fire, but also cooking meat. There's a figure burned to a crsip in front of a door, but it's clear that they tried to pick the lock ahead of them but got caught in the same trap we did at the entrance. [[Costi]] doesn't want to get cooked, [[Rez]] answers she can do it just fine, [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] supercedes and asks [[Eirian|E]] to send help - she sends [[Vask]]. That jerks makes [[Rez]] admit he's better at lockpicking, and magic mouthed it for keeps too. [[Vask]] gets in, and after locking us out for a minute, lets us in. 

Inside is a nice office with wall of windows to the bottom of the well where they could observe the draconic abominations. Pinned to wall are nailed hands, feet, and wings of chromatic wyrmlings and young dragons. [[Freya]] looks at the fire for burned papers, [[Hugh|Finn]] for secret passages - who finds a trap door. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] searches the desk, finding it unlocked and full of notes. It belonged to [[Pleetos Alentas]], at least until recently, though more recent correspondence (the last 4.5 months) has been addressed to [[Nebrur Sunbreaker]], who we're able to deduce it was the barbarian.  He corresponded recently with [[Kelwin Gambletimble]] and [[Tunchon Agira]]. There's also two pristine scales we've found yet, one gold and one brass. We also find a heavy inlaid porcelain or ivory key hidden there, it's engraved with [[Pleetos Alentas]] initials. [[Rez]] has her summoned dragons dissolve the wall of 'trophies'. [[Costi]] sends [[Samuel]] to check out the trap door, it leads to a door in the outer area of the compound. 

[[Zalvaris|Zalv]] found enough food to feed a couple hundred people, and [[Zmaj]] found the treasury. We agree to donate the food and money back to the [[Thelran Asari]] city, along with the [[Platinum Sanctuary of Thelran Asari]]. 

Outside of the escape hatch, [[Pik]] and [[Pak]] have killed 5 people, and have one person tied up, ready for interrogation.
