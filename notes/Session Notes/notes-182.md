# Session 182

We begin with [[Hugh|Finn]], [[Costi]], and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] cleansing the wagon wheel, the last of the items we've found. 

We ask if we could walk around without drawing attention outside the outpost, [[Jahlrak]] says before the last five days it would've been more notable but might be fine for most we pass now. Many are like him, before the scalpel, and are mainly focused on what binds them here. He believes the Castellan [[Strictford]] is tied to most who are bound here. 

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] talks a bit about how we could try to blend in, realizing everything will identify we're living. Maybe our best bet is to pretend to be the next wave of [[Everpresent]] bodies? 

[[Costi]] puzzles on the unique nature of the type of undead [[Jahlrak]] is. In the same way that most are created against their will, he's trapped here. His soul is also severed from his body, but as he holds the scalpel, the overwhelming sense of abhorrent behavior is tampered by a sense of purity too. Fundamentally, he's not that different from a zombie created against their will - unlike the [[Alhoon]] who chose this. [[Samuel]] is not that into him.

We discuss how to travel out of [[The Outpost]], there's two paths: through and below. [[Jahlrak]]'s been warned not to travel below due something there being "untamed". We decide hide in numbers, move through the outpost and deal with anyone we meet as needed. [[Jahlrak]] shares that he no longer wants to die, and thinks he finally feels the drive, and the chance, to start to atone. 

[[Aisha]] pulls [[Malachi]] aside for a check in. She wants his gut check on if he trusts [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]], after some teeth pulling he lands on yes. Then [[Aisha]] asks for his promise not to tune out if he has to hear more really bad news - he agrees. 

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] has a bit of a conversation about if [[Malachi]] should choose to stay here, and if [[F'rocidi]] might've sent him here on purpose. [[Hugh|Finn]] worries about [[Malachi]] giving his life away needlessly, [[Rez]] tries to argue he can decide his own risk, but makes sure he understands just how bad these dunamancy living batteries are. We suggest maybe cleansing him of being [[woetouched]], if we can manage. [[Malachi]] says, this time, he feels like he chose to be here, and maybe should be. [[Rez]] brings up if someone in [[The Shadowfell]] gave him that weapon, and his [[woetouched]], they could maybe be pretty pissed if we try to remove it. [[Malachi]] ends with holding on trying to fully clear it, but gives [[Costi]] permission to stab him, in the attempt to cleanse him, should it become relevant. 

[[Hugh|Finn]] casts commune, and asks [[TrueSilver]]:
* Would [[Malachi]] being a follower of yours save him from being turned to a portal to [[The Shadowfell]]
	* No
* Can I remove a [[woetouched]] without them dying 
	* Rarely, and not without great risk.
* Can you provide some cover for us as we move through [[The Shadowfell]]
	* I wish that I could.

As we leave, [[Rez]] and [[Aisha]] message briefly, saying she can't let [[Malachi]] be turned into a portal, [[Aisha]] says "I know".
