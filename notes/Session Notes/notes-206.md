# Session 206

[[Costi]] shares his hesitations with working with [[Below the Wall]], with his family being the most vulnerable to their threats. We also recognize they might be stronger or more connected than we'd previously considered. [[Rez]] and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] work through a few possible outcomes but we don't land on any agreement. We want to know the duration of our agreement, and what their deal with the slaad was/is. 

[[Freya]] and [[Rawley]] stitch-talk to plan to stitch-talk. She checks in on him, and he shares that he's struggling some with the people and pressures of town. [[Freya]] kinda says fair but you aren't handling things as well as you should be. She says he can rely on his, and he counters they are -- but [[Freya]] calls out he seems pretty mad about it. [[Rawley]] says what we did in [[Nervart]] seemed to really help [[Pelor]], and it's likely connected to exactly when [[Baelos]] was taken. He says [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] is doing great. But he pushes back to remind that the [[Loombrace Cenacle]] has done a lot before now, stopped 15 or 20 of their plans, and the fact that nothing is coming forward now is concerning. There's hundreds of them here and only 6 of the [[Loombrace Cenacle]]. He's also got some pride and anger of [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] being dismissive of all they've done. [[Freya]] sets a boundary against [[Rawley]] throwing [[Julian Slater-Runehorn|Julian]] in [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]'s past. He agrees, and says it was below him. [[Freya]] pushes that we need to be on the same team. 

[[Freya]] talks to [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] about how [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] talks to [[Rawley]]. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] is just as condescending about [[Rawley]] as he was about her but agrees to try to do better to be the bigger person.

[[Freya]] flags down [[Lirnis Kilzire]] to ask for his help tracking down [[Caleb Schleuse]]. He agrees and sends a courier, Veri. He comes back, and specifically misnames [[Rez]] and [[Costi]]. He says he hired [[Wayfinders]] and did make it to [[The Shadowfell]] for the first official trip. He wants interplanar travel to other planes, and confirmed they'd also been to [[City of Brass]] in the past. He'd like to visit the [[The City of Endless Night]] too. When [[Caleb Schleuse]] meets [[Molly]], he immediately knew he was a [[Mimsy]]. [[Freya]] tries to show him a trick, and [[Caleb Schleuse]] gives him a piece of hair to eat. [[Molly]] regurgitates it and throws it into [[My Hole]]. We're led up to his room. 

There he gives us a preview his presentation, and we learn he also has been to the [[the Feywild]]. He's going to publish his current research project and paper on his trip to [[The Shadowfell]], as commissioned by [[Gulsindip Desindever|Superior Desindever]]. Shares he's a friend of [[Wutherford Odlinstin]]. They've co-written a few papers and both studied at [[The Slavaadi College]]. It's clear that he's entirely focused on [[Freya]], pushing some specific people that he knows that [[Freya]] should note. 

[[Molly]] does a quick (dizzit-enhanced) run to register [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] at [[The Rumble]]. Through some miscommunications and also maybe intentionally fucking with someone, it gets done. [[Vy]] comes by with a blue tiefling, seemingly noting [[Molly]]. 

We wait outside the [[Temple of Moradin in Thelran Asari]] and spot [[Kelpi]] loitering from [[Below the Wall]]. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] messages him our two questions: he responds, essentially our debt to them will be done in three days, but the reward discussed was intended to be more open-ended. Nothing will be requested after the convention but they will still owe us a favor to be called in. Regarding their plans for slaad in the [[Songside]] - chaos. We give them response of yes. He says we can learn more immediately. 

[[Kelpi]] has changed appearance to show he's [[Alvar]]. On the third day of the [[Wayfinder Annual Gathering]], just before the Council chair's speech, we need to leave a specific door open -- one fairly close to the tower that has access to speaking platform. That will give us a blank check favor, if they fail, we get another. The [[Thelran Asari Council]] [[Chairman Rascryn Lemblehamble]] is democratic, mostly seen as playing into the benefits of the upper city, but not outright evil. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] asks if we need to leave afterwards, trying to gauge their plans, [[Alvar]] denies any mortal peril and teases he'll have "interesting things to say". [[Alvar]] gives [[Rez]] the diadem [[Eirian]], and gives a bonus pouch to smooth things over. It has 50 platinum in it. When we ask how he did it, [[Willum]] steps out of the fireplace. He apologizes for how the [[Fire Raisers]] deal ended, claiming he didn't know. It seems mostly genuine? He maybe knew more than he's letting on but does seem put off by their final plans. He's sold some stuff in [[Thelran Asari]] before linking up with [[Alvar]]. [[Willum]] claims he thought [[Winifey|Winnie]] was a psychopath, [[Relakas]] was a freak (ate someone once), and thinks [[Cinder]] fused his body with an efreet prince and is some sort of elemental creature now. In the [[Fire Raisers]] conflict he made 5 [[refined firestone]]s and then sold them - one to [[Alvar]]. They'll give us the exact location of the door in a note that we should burn. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] asks who will fill his slot on the [[Thelran Asari Council]] - they prefer it's not a [[Goodbarrels]], Wilco is a potential, Gen. Radden is the worst, Srendex is an intriguing option.
