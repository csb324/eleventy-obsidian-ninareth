# Session 184

[[Costi]] quickly looks through the bodies, and doesn't find much - including any other [[purple dunamantic stones]]. We give out ours, one to [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] and one to [[Hugh|Finn]]. We check the door for traps.

Entering into the town hall, there's a shade at the front desk. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] casts locate object on the [[Cenacle Oak Box]], she gets the sense of forward and up, about 200ish feet. We tell them we have an appointment with [[His Eminence]], but learn he won't be in for a while, we fib that Mr. [[Jabokav]] told us to wait upstairs. She agrees, telling us to stay out of the way, pointing us through the doors on either side of the stairs. 

After some hesitation, and an awkward wait at the top of the stairs, we're let into the top floor by a creature in his mid-twenties in black leathers, with a purple cloak, and dark hair in a ponytail. He seems tickled about something.

Inside, there a number of humanoids, and a number of NOT humanoids. They simply say "[[Synchrona]]". We immediately fight, with a sentinel, a cherub, a halfling, and several undead. 

In the back of the room, [[Dolrak]] is a desiccating body in a metal entrapment with purple stones running up the sides. His chest is ripped open, with nothing inside. It's horrific.

It's unbearably brutal. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], [[Aisha]], and [[Freya]] all end up at the same time. [[Hugh|Finn]] uses a [[purple dunamantic stones]] to save [[Rez]] from a dispel to turn her into a boulder. [[Panyas Rivereth]] is revealed to have two gold fingers. [[Malachi]] takes out [[Dolrak]], destroying the contraption making him a portal. Our way out is closed. [[Malachi]] is nearly overtaken by the shadows. [[Costi]] gets targeted protecting [[Malachi]] but after several saves he ends up grappled by the dunamantic battery-chair. 

[[Freya]] is brought down, despite the [[Everpresent]] clearly saying that wasn't the goal. The Sentinel tries to plane shift with her dead body, but [[The Curator]] pops into her mind, asking if she'd like her to intervene - [[Freya]] says she told her not to die here. [[The Curator]] agrees, saying the Sentinel is bad news, and does a 9th level counterspell. 

[[Jahlrak]] finds the [[Cenacle Oak Box]], and gives it to us. [[Hugh|Finn]] grabs [[Panyas Rivereth]] bag of holding, [[Rez]] grabs the [[purple dunamantic stones]] bracelet off a body, [[Costi|Cost-ape]] breaks the chair, [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] tells [[Jahlrak]] that he's ready and able.
