# Session 202

As the energy to dispel flows out of [[Raven's Edge]] and into this beacon, the darkness globs over the light. [[Freya]] gets a weird [[There's Plainly Something Wrong with the Planes]] tingle. Fog covers all the openings, trapping [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]]. the pillars suddenly show mirrors, thick roiling shadow also covers the mirror faces. 

Looking at one of the mirrors, there's writing on the bottom that is hard to make out. "Exuberant, boisterous expression" is on the one by [[Costi]], he attempts to make that face into the mirror, nothing happens.

The one by [[Rhynilex Svenik|Rhyn]] reads, "beauty's sharpest prick", [[Rez]] reads, "Comfort's restful space" - she learns we can angle the mirrors and tries to move it to face where the beds were below. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] hits it and it gives off a sense of anger when she uses divine smite. [[Jade]] reads "hearty, homey nourishment"

[[Freya]] gets some special attack from [[The Weaver]] when the larger, twisted shadow creature crawls out of the orb. 

We finally remember to check the fifth pillar, seeing a compass rose on it, and are able to figure out the puzzle: 

"hearty, homey nourishment" - Stew - west 
"beauty's sharpest prick" - Thorn - north 
"Comfort's restful space" - Seat - east 
"Exuberant, boisterous expression" - Shout - south

Turning the mirrors the right way forces us out of our strange inter-planar location and allows [[Pelor]]'s light to shine through!
