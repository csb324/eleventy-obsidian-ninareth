# Session 195

[[Freya]] messages to [[Rawley]] to tell him the priest moot went well and to check off that it's okay to tell [[Fimbeth Udovarax]] and [[Umenelis Wocornalkint]]. He wants as few people as possible to know exactly what's happening. He suggests sharing the threat with as few tactile details as possible. He continues to be worried anyone we tell will let it out. 

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] has a spirited debate on how much is smart to tell and where we put people at risk versus helping. 

We head to the [[Rooted Grove]] to give [[Fimbeth Udovarax]] and [[Umenelis Wocornalkint]] the download. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] gives a deeply inspirational nod to the young fighting prodigy as he's training some of the [[The Order of the Blue Rose]] fighters.

As we enter the chamber, it's empty, except for [[Fimbeth Udovarax]], [[Umenelis Wocornalkint]], and surprisingly [[Willow]]. He tastes a leaf and tells us to look in [[My Hole]]. Turns out there's a [[Molly]] in there. [[Willow]] knows a friend of his, [[Flabbertstagen]]. [[Willow]] is surprised we don't know what we've got here. Turns out [[Flabbertstagen]] is his, he made him in his dreams. He found him in [[the Feywild]], when a couple of guys like [[Molly]] pulled him out with hope ropes, made from their hopes. They had a demiplane they existed in. Some people call them [[Kalashtar]] or [[Mimsy]]. 

[[Willow]] helps us interrogate [[Molly]] under zone of truth. [[Willow]] believes that if he's only been here 17 days, he likely doesn't fully understand lying. [[Willow]] has a brilliant idea then, mixes some powders and items to drink some concoction and knocks himself unconscious. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] brings him back but he complains he needs more time. [[Freya]] clocks him, but then [[Costi]] standing by gives him a nat 20 to pop right back up. He's annoyed, and [[Molly]] wops him back into unconsciousness. Oops, he went too hard and dies (nat 1). [[Costi]] picks another future, oops he dies in that one too (another nat 1), but then eventually brings him back to existence. Apparently it's easier to talk to [[Flabbertstagen]] in the liminal space between life and death. He vouches for [[Molly]] - works for us!

[[Willow]] sings himself a chair in the art of the [[Gia'Thoren]]. [[Umenelis]] gives us a read out on the [[Priest Moot]], it does seem like we found a genuine threat to the city. [[Pelor]] order is policing the street, [[Moradin]] are trying to get information from the workers, the [[Stalwart Shields]] are prepping for battle and [[The Wayward Wardens]] are prepping healing. [[Corellon Larethian]] and [[Bahamut]] forces are working on containing the slaad. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] gives the warning that what we share puts them, and those close to them at high personal risk. We share the basics of who the [[Everpresent]] is, what they want, and their symbol. That they have forces throughout the continent in positions of power, including on the [[Thelran Asari Council]], and within both of their organizations. [[Rez]] shares how they are tied to the [[Unscaled]], and we show them a [[purple dunamantic stones]] a [[Node of Arcane Displacement]] - [[Fimbeth Udovarax]] keeps one. We share about earning gold, dunamancy ties, and [[Alhoon]]s. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] shares about [[Wyn Elreal]] and his ties to similar experiences in [[New Fairhaven]]. We share about [[feytouched]] and [[woetouched]], and [[There's Plainly Something Wrong with the Planes]]. We share the four implicated gods: [[Pelor]], [[Corellon Larethian]], [[Bahamut]], and [[Moradin]]. We speak about the [[Army of Undeath]] and the [[flesh stitched behemoth]]. We share about the role of celestials and dodging the [[god pact]]. There's an oracle in the [[Order of the Platinum Dragon]] last seen in [[Durmask]]. We also flag pirates are involved somehow. [[Rez]] warns about the [[blue dragons]] and [[Fimbeth Udovarax]] says there was recently an inspection, 9 months ago, but they could try to manufacture another one sooner than the next few years, [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] suggests the weapons from [[G.T.]] - we offer to write a report on it. 

At [[Fimbeth Udovarax]]'s request, [[Rez]] shares her true self with [[Umenelis]]. She then gets a glass of true sight and lets [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] look too. 

They ask what they can do, we ask for them to help us find information, to ready their orders in the ways they can, and from [[Rez]], to root out the corruption within their orders. We make our leave. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] pulls [[Molly]] aside for a chat. She tries to help him understand both what a secret is and how important these are. She pushes him that there's no babysitter in [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] any more, so [[Molly]] has to hold it together and she needs his help to do that. This makes [[Molly]] cry, happy tears?
