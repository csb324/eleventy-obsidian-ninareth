# Session 198

When [[Molly]] pulls the stopwatch past his nose, he gets a whiff of old coffee hinting at his creator.

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] heads to the [[Thelran Asari Society]], we debate how to best get [[Costi]]'s humanizing piece accepted. He considers amending his final piece and decides against it. 

[[Molly]] heads over early to settle into a hiding spot. He sees [[Tarold]] failing to be bribed to vote on a piece, two others seem more susceptible however. Verible is pitching his race science piece. He mind links with [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] when she arrives as "Jennifer", along with [[Costi]] as "Zer" and [[Rez]]. 

This meeting is far more tense and formal, giving off a business meeting air, with cliques supporting different key members, [[Kristeean]] being one. [[Bella]] and [[Niklas]] are also present. [[Costi]] shares his title, [[Immigration in Thelran Asari: Jobs Taken, Lives Stolen]]. 

Outside, [[Freya]] sees [[Gimzid]] walking by the bar multiple times. Notices himself being noticed by her, and says "So you're ugh, not interested in the journal?" He says he wants to take a walk with [[Freya]], saying [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] can reach out to her. What should be a shake down is instead a baffling conversation with vomiting, boob adjustments, and a tentative agreement. 

They walk into the [[Songside]], where he's arranged a guard post to be empty. As she's about to follow him down a hidden tunnel, [[Costi]] gets a gut feeling and sendings to [[Freya]], "Had a bad feeling, everything alright?", she replies, "Yea nope, I'm with [[Gimzid]], vomited on him, now in Songside, going through statue of guy with harp". 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] sends [[Molly]], [[Costi]] gives [[Samuel]] a bead containing arcane eye to go along with.  

Top six include "Zer", [[Tarold]], and [[Bella]]. No teeth, thankfully. "Zer"'s piece gets voted as the cover story, with the back cover counter is the decaying body. Music is included, and they're cutting out the fourth to instead add more [[Kristeean]] poetry. 

In the hideout, [[Gimzid]] shares that this is a safehouse and that he was worried our group would not have all joined, but that [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] will come if [[Freya]] is in danger. That is NEWS to [[Freya]], who continues to be the absolute worst. [[Gimzid]] is heavily scarred, clearly an experienced fighter. They play cards to pass the time. 

The rest of us meet up, discussing a [[Eberronian Military]] themed cover story along the way. As we arrive at the entrance, it opens for us. Two humans join once inside. First, a man, [[Alvar]] in an outfit meant to be insane, clearly someone who changes his appearance a lot, wearing [[refined firestone]]. Second, a woman named [[Kindy]] with a crossbow and pistol. Their organization is called [[Below the Wall]], and they offer an "arrangement" for our help. [[Alvar]] claims he wants to work on behalf of the new districts, outside the walled city, and are exploring multiple ways for that to be achieved. They've learned some of our work in [[Eberron]], including our "tragic", inability to stop the [[Duchess v'Dharinov]] from being killed. Plus our efforts to help the [[Fire Raisers]] until it was inconvenient.  When pressed on his sources, he says "us rebels all talk" - maybe [[Cinder]]? Other [[Fire Raisers]]? 

He wants to offer help for our help, not fully trusting each other. [[Alvar]] says he'd share more information once we begin, but wants to ensure our partnership lasts slightly longer. He warns that if something happen to him, or other of their members, after our partnership begins, they'd go after our families. 

[[Rez]] asks where [[Alvar]] got his [[refined firestone]], he says he got from a friend recently, a mutual friend. We don't necessarily lock that down. 

We challenge them to finding another way to get in touch with us as we decide if there's a benefit here. He wishes us luck on our trip, somehow knowing about our upcoming [[Nervart]] journey. 

[[Costi]] sends to [[Costi's mom]], "Hey mom hope you're okay! The revolutionary group in TA [[Below the Wall]], know of them? they seem to know of me.", she replies, "Uhm, [[Below the Wall]], that might be the network we've talked to. I'll ask."
