# Session 192

We start the morning with a trip to the [[Rooted Grove]] at [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]'s request, she wants [[Molly]] to get looked at and predictably, we all tag along. 

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] debates how to get everyone other than [[Rez]] to the [[Thelran Asari Society]] meeting. [[Rez]] plans to ask [[Tarold]] to pre-game, have [[Costi]] and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] coincidentally walk in, and make them so likable they get invited too. [[Freya]] and [[Molly]] will be spying nearby. 

We reach the [[Rooted Grove]], where [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] asks if there's any researcher who can help understand who, or what, or anything else on [[Molly]]'s deal. 

[[Rez]] sendings to [[Tarold]], "Hi! I know we've got your Society thing tonight and I while I'm totally excited it's also too far from now. Drinks? Earlier? Bar nearby??" He replies, "Sure! How about the Lousy Hound? It’s pretty close. 2 hours before". 

[[Costi]] coaxes [[Molly]] to join him and [[Freya]] to the pie shop. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] goes to spend some time playing and communing, but stops to try and help improve a group doing martial drills, unfortunately poorly. They're happy to demonstrate for her, but it's at best, average. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] takes the commander and the one who performed best, she's harsh but exacting and clearly focused on being sure they learn. 

At the commanders respect, [[Rez]] both has some unexpected possessive feelings, and she even notices that she has that thought, which is both weird and new. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] spots her, but in a turn of events, despite [[Rez]]'s sheepish confession to watching, from a bush, Adrienne doesn't tell her to leave! They discuss [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]'s new "guys", [[Rez]] offers to ask [[Zmaj]] for tips leading, and goes to watch [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] play harp. With permission!

In this meditation, [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] thinking about all of her responsibilities and the need for help, she seems to have some magical enhancing ability for those under her command. [[Rez]] sees her getting caught up in her art, emotions building as a tear runs down her cheek, as the plants around her start to move. Vines come down and knit together before transforming into a gleaming white horse, with a blue rose on her muzzle. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] found her steed!! Welcome [[Bluebell]]! She and [[Rez]] ride back to the hotel -- this is fine, this is normal, friends can share a horse, no one is having any feelings about it and *certainly* no one understands those feelings.

Meanwhile, [[Costi]], [[Freya]], and [[Molly]] eat pie. Well, that is until [[Freya]] and [[Costi]] start arguing - which is the perfect time for [[Molly]] to give them the slip. But he just went to steal a pie. 

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] all comes back together and meets [[Bluebell]]! We discuss our plans to infiltrate the [[Thelran Asari Society]] at the [[The Fifth Page]] in the [[Songside]]. It's near [[The Seven Copper Opera]]. 

[[Molly]] gets let into [[The Fifth Page]] by [[Freya]] and finds a hidden spot to wait for the meeting. During the wait, [[Molly]] overhears two beautiful young people, [[Bella]] and [[Kristeean]]. There's bad poetry, and sloppy making out. A half-elf in a guard captain outfit, [[Niklas]], who seems to in a leadership role. 

[[Costi]], [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], and [[Freya]] hang out. They change their hairstyles, temporarily, and debate making those changes more permanent. 

[[Rez]] goes to meet [[Tarold]], he introduces her to the bartender [[Byrn]]. They meet every two weeks, between 15-30 most of the time. It's more of an artist salon? They're putting together a journal of ideas, wanting to debate and share their perspectives. His best friend, an asshole poet, only does them for the live performance, as they're only written for his voice. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] "Jennifer" and [[Costi]] "Zer" come in and play it all off fine. [[Tarold]] invites them over. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] gets almost recognized by [[Bella]], who plays it off as her sister. She says [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] "redefined" what's possible on a harp, and she's heard she's an adventurer, and is in town right? She's been seen at the [[The Silver Tongue]]. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] "Jennifer" deflects to her growing obsession with [[Corellon Larethian]] and says her performances are not really as planned now. [[Bella]] begs for her to let her know if anything comes up soon. She then plays a version of [[Millik]]'s Battle March. 

[[Gimzid]], a grizzled muscled halfling, walks in. [[Niklas]], a half-elf with a dark ponytail, is introduced to us by [[Tarold]]. He seems unfortunately somewhat decent? Who knows. They plan to release their first edition of the journal at the [[Wayfinder Annual Gathering]]. They're very excited to be challenging the general population, and hope outrage will get broader engagement. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] goes over to compliment [[Bella]] while she's talking to [[Gimzid]]. He lives in the [[Gears]], and intros in talking about the need for more green space, like the [[Inner City]]. 

[[Rez]] and [[Costi]] try to talk about [[Zalev]] and harsher discipline, hoping to gauge how violent [[Gimzid]] is. Something gets to him but it's unclear what. 

[[Kristeean]] does a performance reading, and seems to magically move [[Costi]], [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], and [[Molly]]. [[Rez]] is aware that it was a mass charm person, quite powerful. It's clear [[Gimzid]] clocked that as well. 

[[Molly]] clocks several things through the rest of the meeting:
* that [[Tarold]] and [[Bella]] have a tense conversation, with her saying "I won't say this again".
* [[Gimzid]] has a fairly long conversation with [[Niklas]], they shake hands and exchange a note. They didn't seem to know each other tonight. 
* [[Bella]] looks at [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] a lot. Fuck. 

At the end of the event, [[Gimzid]] invites [[Rez]] to another "cultural club" with their own ideas. He asks if she can telepathically communicate and leaves with that. She agrees. 

[[Tarold]] says we're officially members and invites us to their upcoming emergency meeting to vote on the final inclusions to the journal. 

On our way home, we hear a roar of some sort of creature. [[Freya]] notes that [[Gimzid]] had walked this way as he left.
