# Session 181

We pause at the precipice of [[The Shadowfell]], trying to figure out if there's any planning possible. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] asks [[Rez]] what she knows, she says to be quiet, avoid drawing attention (as heartbreaking as that is), and stay chill. Plus, if [[Kaycee Teapetal|Rez's Mom]] is nearby then we have to say hi. [[Costi]] asks [[Samuel]] if there's anything else to flag, but he's unhelpful, as per usual. 

[[Malachi]] steps up to the portal, and tries to sense anything there, but nothing can pull through or past the small amount of pain he feels in his scar. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] hesitates, telling [[Rez]] that last time she went through one of these portals, she died. [[Rez]] says she's brought her back once. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] scoffs she can't count on that again, [[Rez]] counters that, actually, she can. 

As we enter [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] feels like we're forcing our way through shadows that cut like glass. Our emotions are in turmoil as we land. 

Once we get our bearings, we see a room extremely similar to the room we just left on the [[Prime Material]], except for signs of habitation. There's a human man, writing into a notebook, as we enter. Nondescript black leather clothes, and immediately after spotting us begins to cast sending. 

[[Rez]] counterspells him as [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] and [[Malachi]] attack, [[Freya]] grapples him with a hand over his mouth. We quickly tie him up. [[Freya]] ties him up while [[Malachi]] and [[Costi]] rifle through his clothes - they find a key ring and a components pouch. [[Hugh|Finn]] listens for anyone else who heard our entrance. [[Aisha]] and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] look at the notes on the wall, recognizing some sort of alchemical and arcane formula, that seems like it's trying to do complicated conjuration magic with dunamancy influence. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] takes a beat to question why [[Aisha]] even knows what that is. 

[[Rez]] moves to ask him who he was sending to, aided by [[Malachi]] holding a waiting punch to his temple. His says his name is [[Adrith Cornsyth]]. 

He says he was sending to his boss, nobody told him more were coming through. We bluff that we need to reprimand whoever dropped the line of communication. 

After a number of threats, he shares his boss is named [[Panyas Rivereth]], an [[Elder]]. He say them bring someone with a box through. About half of their force, 20, has been confined to the [[Bogwood Outpost]]. The rest are in the town, which is beautiful at night. They've been here for 5 days. He confirms they all work for the [[Everpresent]], was promised riches and power, doing desk work for a year, and then brought them here. The rest are more "true believers", he's not sure he likes it. 

His job is to be sure no one enters through the portal, and to watch if the beacon is lit in the [[Pelor]] temple, in which case they're all supposed to head to the town hall, by the tavern. The stone building is barracks. 

He says the mission of the [[Everpresent]] has been a bit hidden, but he thinks their goal is "peace, prosperity, and conquering death". They want to "end it all", he didn't ask a lot of details. 

Leadership is all over, he's from [[Durmask]] but has been to [[Tinkate]], [[Thelran Asari]], [[Mulfara]], [[Quaath]]. 

We could hide in some storage rooms downstairs, there's no phrases to know each other, we could be part of the crew to come in. When asked about how they're handling [[The Shadowfell]], he shows an amulet that helps keep his emotions in check. 

[[Elder]] [[Panyas Rivereth]] is top dog, reports directly to "[[His Eminence]]", he can do strong magic, and well connected mage in [[Prime Material]], he sounds south. There's also someone from "[[Her Majesty]]" here as well, who carries a sword. [[Panyas Rivereth]] has "earned a bit of gold". He think [[Dolrak]] is in the town hall, was cracked open to power the portal. 

We debate, [[Costi]] suggests he could be a mole, [[Adrith Cornsyth]] willing offers body parts we could take to ensure he can't speak (tongue, or hands?). Many of us don't believe we can ensure he won't betray us. 

As we talk, footsteps pound up the steps, and we throw [[Adrith Cornsyth]] in [[My Hole]]. [[Malachi]] grabs a hobgoblin and pulls him in, recognizing that the shadows pull to him the same way. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] recognizes that he's the hobgoblin who tortured [[Millik]], he begs if we "brought it, and begs to touch it". As he does, he seems to stop fighting off something and gain some clarity. He immediately begins sobbing. 

He warns someone is coming very soon, and we need to stay hidden, more of the living. If they're our enemies, they're his. He suggests we make it appear like [[Adrith Cornsyth]] is sleeping, and hide to over take the next round of guards. The hobgoblin, [[Jahlrak]], says there are places that are safe for the living. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] recognizes his last name is the same as the founder of [[Haag Kitot]]. 

[[Freya]] snaps [[Adrith Cornsyth]]'s neck, placing him so that he appears asleep at the desk. [[Rez]] is given the [[Amulet of Calm]], and does in fact feel better. 

We easily overtake the new guard in a few seconds, taking a second [[Amulet of Calm]]. They only abate our mental status while they're worn. [[Rez]], with [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]'s help, makes a perfect copy of the notes on the wall. [[Freya]] and [[Malachi]] hide the bodies, [[Costi]] roots through for anything on them, taking an identical key ring, component pouch. They both have notable tattoos, the half orc has 7, [[Adrith Cornsyth]] only 3, but they both have an [[Freya's Mysterious Circles|Eye of the Everpresent]] tattoo on the back of their necks. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] and [[Hugh|Finn]] they determine that [[Jahlrak]] is genuinely trying to help us. [[Freya]] takes those patches of skin. Remembering the warning that "where goes their eye there goes their protection", we hid the scalped tattoos in a lead box. 

[[Jahlrak]] leads us onwards, and through a passing window, we see a very different version of [[Bogwood Outpost]] - 50 or 60 buildings, including homes, with no marshland in sight. Mostly humanoids, but with some unnatural creatures lingering throughout. 

As we enter into the safe room [[Jahlrak]] leads us to, [[Aisha]] and [[Costi]] analyze the tablet compared to the sigils on the walls, looking in particular to the sigils surrounding us. It's extremely advanced and experimental magic, like the [[necromantic spikes]]. The amount of magic involved is very intense. It requires some sort of catalyst, a super charged component. After some more digging, we learn the catalyst, specifically for here, it needed a person who was woe touched. uh-oh. The death of said catalyst will permanently close the portal. It's clear that this spell opens a permanent portal to [[The Shadowfell]]. 

Meanwhile, the rest of us talk to [[Jahlrak]]. He shares he was not always of [[The Shadowfell]], and removes his [[Iron Shadows]] mask to show he's an older teen hobgoblin. He fought in a war against [[Thelran Asari]], died early in it. He's from a well regarded family. His group was stationed at the [[Bogwood Outpost]], in charge of interrogating prisoners. He tortured [[Millik]], before a group ([[The Zumbinis]]) entered and killed him. [[Strictford]] found him, and now he's stuck here. He's saying he's not choosing to be here. [[Kulrak]] was his uncle, and founded [[Haag Kitot]]. He shares that since then, each death of a hobgoblin has been described to him as torture. He blames himself for all of that pain. 

His earnestness persuades [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]], and [[Rez]] agrees to work with him on the condition that if they meet [[Kaycee Teapetal|Rez's Mom]] he has to apologize to her. He agrees. 

[[Jahlrak]] says they all arrived about 5 days ago, before that there were no living here. A halfling was the first here, who had a finger of gold. They have an elf bound, he had no shirt, his chest was stitched together. 

We hand out the cleansed items:
scalpel - [[Rez]]
pelor scepter - [[Hugh|Finn]]
harp - [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]
hair pin - [[Freya]]
wagon wheel - [[Costi]]

[[Rez]] and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] agree we should try to determine if we're near the [[The City of Endless Night]], but disagree on if we should try to go there. 

[[Hugh|Finn]] has a sweet moment with [[Jahlrak]], connecting over taking actions their not proud of and sharing that everyone deserves to find peace. He asks what our aims are here, and if we are here to help people - if so, he wants [[Hugh|Finn]] to use him as a tool. [[Hugh|Finn]] will not agree, knowing what it's like to have been a tool of violence. [[Jahlrak]] says he will follow [[Hugh|Finn]] to amend the impact of his actions across history. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] asks [[Costi]] to try and tease out why [[Aisha]] knows so much about dunamancy. It was independent research, learning from her gun, which was a gift. From [[Malachi]], who got it from pirates. He killed an took it off pirates in [[Breakers Bay]], far enough away from [[Copper Cove]] and [[Grandor's Queen]] to cause some concern. Turns out [[Schrayol Vactderdin]] was the Captain of that ship. We say maybe we can use it to scry on him once we're back at the [[Prime Material]]. 

[[Jahlrak]] says it seems like [[Dolrak]] also had shadows leaking out of him in the same torturous manner. 

[[Rez]] has a moment where she sees if her [[Shadowfell Shard]] feels the same on this plane, it maybe doesn't seem as cold. 

[[Freya]] reaches out to [[The Curator]], but nothing seems to break through, at first. After a long delay, they do break through. Yes, they know she's in [[The Shadowfell]]. No, they don't know if her blood would be more weird here. Yes, it would not be good to die here. 

We discuss our plan, we loosely say: getting the [[Cenacle Oak Box]] is our primary goal, we leave through [[Hugh|Finn]] once it's done. The more info we get the better, and we won't stay to fight but we'll go through whoever we need to. If [[Dolrak]] is being used as another tortured dunamantic battery, we should kill him. If we can bring him with us, we will. 

Agreeing, [[Jahlrak]] says lead us out of the [[Bogwood Outpost]].
