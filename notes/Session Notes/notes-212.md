# Session 212

With a little bit of time, both [[Costi]] and [[Rez]] are able to eventually see past the lure of the harpy song. Trying to decide what to do, we decide have [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] go ahead and cast locate object. Nothing pings within 1000 feet of where we are right now. 

We all try to puzzle out any hints. [[Rez]] is able to feel that yes, there is a similar territorial encroachment that is normal when nearing another dragons hoard. [[Molly]] tries to smell for treasure. Suddenly he gets a smell of apricot and brandy.  [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] tries to think about anyone that [[Julian Slater-Runehorn|Julian]] knew favored apricot brandy but doesn't come up with anyone. [[Molly]] tries to remember any wayfinder who smells that way, and categorically knows that there isn't, but it calls to him despite that. [[Costi]] recalls that harpies really aren't all that tough, and [[Rez]] locks in on the sense of tracking any dragons that like to burrow or tunnel in the sand. 

We approach the oasis and [[Freya]] and [[Molly]] make soo short work of the harpies. The illusion of the oasis melts away to lava, blasted sand glass, and a crevice [[Rez]] spots as the entrance to a tunnel. [[Freya]] pounds our way through the ground to open up enough of an entrance for us to squeeze through. Meanwhile, [[Costi]] searches the bodies of the harpies and finds and finds mostly junk and a metallic dragon scale - we confirm it's brass. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] brings up a mind blowing fact that maybe the creatures here are real, since demiplanes don't mean illusory. This sends [[Rez]] on a bit of a spiral. [[Rez]] says we could be in for a good time, and we all look for cool stuff to gift, [[Rez]] makes some of the nicest possible apricot brandy. 

As we enter, [[Costi]] gets more of the same blib feeling, and is able to connect that the feeling was more similar to the [[Sourandi's Lighthouse]]. Beginning to walk, [[Molly]] warns something about this encounter is wrong, and just doesn't make sense. 

Deeper into the cavern, [[Molly]] starts to get a stronger scent of blood. We turn a corner and see a large underground chamber with geodes that illuminate the space with an unnatural light. There's a pool in the center of the room, and across the space there is a clear pedestal on which rests an empty plate. Against the side of the room, we see the corpse of an adult brass dragon, it's chest is cut open from throat to tail. Three figures are in the room. One hulking blood covered figure holding a massive scythe wearing iron boots and a purple-steel contraption over the top of his head, with swirling lines of black over his body, with purple stones in every quarter inch of skin, amounting to hundreds. He's beside another figure in long purple red robes with long blonde hair and pinned open eyes with interweaving circles are tattooed around, and in, her eyes, but her mouth is sewn shut and a constantly moving halo of eyes swirl around her hear. The final figure is someone in all golden armor with a shield and sword. 

Good, you've arrived. I'm sorry to say your journey ends here. As he speaks, the tunnel behind us collapses in a purple flash. It's a brutal encounter. We learn that radiant damage stops scythe guys healing but each of his embedded stones allows him to take extra rounds. The eye gal adds an ungodly amount (12) to counterspells.
