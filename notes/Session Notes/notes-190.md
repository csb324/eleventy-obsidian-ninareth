# Session 190

We debate about if we're accompanying [[Hugh|Finn]] to [[Eva Sidorev|Eva]]'s grandchild's musical concert. Deciding to make a drinking game out of it, [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] all agrees. We get ourselves some nice gin and friendship flasks. 

At [[The Academy in Thelran Asari]], we head to one of the smaller theaters and meet [[Costi's Dad|Vladimir Grosu]] and [[Nadia]]. The performances are a mix of genuinely impressive, and painful. [[Eva Sidorev|Eva]]'s grandkid brings out bagpipes. 

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] bickers, as we are want to do, before [[Eva Sidorev|Eva]] finds us with her family. [[Costi's Dad|Vladimir Grosu]] swaps [[Eberronian Military]] stories with [[Eva Sidorev|Eva]], the bagpipe playing kid bonds with [[Nadia]]. The rest of us shoot the shit with her husband [[Heyner]]. 

As we're ready to leave, [[Nadia]] is not in eye sight, [[Costi]] sends to her, and very incoherently tells her we're leaving and to stay with [[Eva Sidorev|Eva]]. [[Hugh|Finn]] also sends to tell her to end up at home, [[Nadia]] plans to go back to the [[The Silver Tongue]]. 

We make our way to [[The Drowning]], all mostly appearing to be normal people, as we are. It's clear that the downsides of industrial complex is impacting this area most. [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] avoids trouble but does notice the discontent. 

[[Costi]] tries to scout out a contact, and pulls [[Rez]] into it. They both try their best to be MAD and uh, [[Costi]] almost gets stabbed but importantly doesn't! That failure prompts us into playing a game. Teams of three, girls vs boys, on who can get the most real information. 

[[Hugh|Finn]] and [[Freya]] scout out a bar [[Pepper and Salt]], that seems like exactly the spot we need. Our two teams make our separate plans. 

Team girls are going to get someone to open up at the bar, giving the space for them to talk and follow wear it goes. [[Freya]] is accidentally intimidating, [[Rez]] tries to find just the right amount of angry, and [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] goes for sad but not heartbroken. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] joins two guys, who've had a shitty couple of weeks, [[Val]] and [[Vano]]. They're having of course, she has to [[Arvlo]], "[[Ar]]", he takes care of people around here but there's been a lot more pressure from folks in the Devs. They don't dislike him, and seem to be going against the [[Goodbarrels]], though they've been supportive of [[The Drowning]] for a number of years, but now there's cloning of their product and offering their incentives and political clout to other neighborhoods, and now they've not been able to price match and had to quit. Maybe their competition has a sweetheart deal with [[Thelran Asari Council]]. Seems like a number of folks in the Devs are having to close up shop. Oops her dad, Lenny, recently died. She's Lenny's girl alright! They saw him 6 months ago, it's been tough. She sells it as she always does. Finds out he's maybe a rough kinda guy. He also complained about the growing pressure between the districts, things are getting out of hand. 

[[Rez]] sees a group of young guys, halfling, human, and a gnome. [[Rez]] ends up joining in the game, turns out he's super racist. Talks a lot about the pure undisturbed culture of [[Thelran Asari]] of the past. Sure he's not "heartless" but people can't just keep pouring in. There's a ordinances passed in some of the districts to close off population growth. Meet every other week, at a bookstore called [[The Fifth Page]]. He's [[Tarold]]. 

[[Costi's Dad|Vladimir Grosu]] tells [[Hugh|Finn]] and [[Costi]] a plan to have [[Costi]] sit by and eavesdrop to find a target, while [[Hugh|Finn]] and him draw the attention. 

Meanwhile, [[Costi's Dad|Vladimir Grosu]] has a heart to heart with [[Hugh|Finn]], saying he has a selfish ask for him. He thanks [[Hugh|Finn]] for how he helped shaped [[Costi]] into the man he's grown to be. He wants [[Hugh|Finn]] to do it again, and help protect and raise [[Nadia]]. [[Hugh|Finn]] seems confused, but seems to agree. They talk of how [[Costi]] is ready to look after himself, but shouldn't be burdened with looking out for another. 

[[Costi]] is distracted being in full on spy mode but hears a group, being almost on purpose too loud, a real anger towards those who live in the walled part of [[Thelran Asari]]. They're named [[Calmop Billowfern]] and [[Gimzid]], and seem right on the precipice of violent actions themselves. This seems to be exactly what we were looking for. 

On the way home, [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] quizzes [[Costi's Dad|Vladimir Grosu]] on how he could possibly connect with [[Nadia]] after all this time. He's very patient with her, correctly sensing there's some layers here. 

[[Hugh|Finn]] approaches [[Costi]] after the rest of [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] goes to bed to tell about [[Costi's Dad|Vladimir Grosu]]'s offer. [[Hugh|Finn]] doesn't want [[Costi]] to feel abandoned, or to die. [[Costi]] shares he's been worried about him, and all the years of war he's has to endure. [[Costi]] is always a sending away. 

[[Hugh|Finn]] goes to tell [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] next, saying he's not coming with us anymore. He's staying here, not with [[Eva Sidorev|Eva]], but with [[Nadia]]. He asks for her to keep [[Costi]] alive, saying she's the only person he can trust with that. He tells [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] he loves her, she gives him a hug. [[Hugh|Finn]] gives her a gift from [[TrueSilver]], a bundle of silver wires, or maybe harp strings? She installs them in the [[Songwarden’s Solace]], [[TrueSilver's Strings]], etching a few notes of the first song she played for [[Hugh|Finn]] as she strums them for the first time. 

[[Hugh|Finn]] tells [[Rez]] and [[Freya]] together, who both are confused but want [[Hugh|Finn]] to take the time he needs. [[Rez]] says his life is long and he has plenty of time ahead of him to do what he wants (and have fun with her later!)

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] is all slow to rise, not sure about starting the day to make it real. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] does an assessment of how everyone feels:
*  [[Costi's Dad|Vladimir Grosu]] has relief when he looks at [[Hugh|Finn]], seeing how he's getting maybe what he needs. But he's anxious about what it means for [[Costi]]. He's not sure if he made the right choice, but is happy for it. 
* [[Costi]] is focused on both [[Costi's Dad]] and [[Hugh|Finn]], he's very confident on this going well, and grateful his dad pulled this off
* [[Rez]] is mostly unbothered, but occasionally is tying her puzzlement over [[Hugh|Finn]] leaving to her puzzlement about [[Rezdrienne]]
* [[Hugh|Finn]] is full of anxious perfectionism
