# Session 201

While [[Freya]] and [[Rez]] revel in the pie-eating, at [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]'s amusement - [[Costi]] and [[Rhynilex Svenik|Rhyn]] have a chat about the excess of the above ground communities, and our strange mix of embarrassing and impressive. 

[[Rez]] tries to subtly ask around about potion suppliers in [[Nervart]], learns that [[Raloberos Dewstar]] "Ralo" has a shop nearby in the more business oriented part of town, learn he's that halfling, on the taller side, 4'1", thick mutton chops and auburn hair, well dressed. He likes to procure his own ingredients, often coming and going from the city -  he used to be an adventurer himself. He's both strong and has some magical capabilities. He seems to be generally well regarded but aloof, no one mentions anyone knowing him personally. He's in town, currently selling special edition berry flavored potions. 

[[Rez]] shares this update with [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]], [[Jade]] and [[Molly]] offer to go stake out and see if they can find anything, accompanied by an arcane eye from [[Costi]]. They go to his store [[Morning Dew]]. They see two people working, one is clearly [[Raloberos Dewstar]] and the other a human woman stocking the shelves in a similar apron. 

[[Molly]] scouts out the general layout of the shop, [[Costi]] then uses his eye to travel through the drains, attempting to get a visual of one of the back spaces. He enters into a room where several drains empty, both clearly below the teaching room, some unidentifiable, and a door on the far side of the wall. There's both bones, unknown type but at least large, and some sparkling powder, with seeming inherent magical quality. 

Behind the door, there's stairs leading up to an air tight heavy locked door, made of metal. He sends the eye back not to the training room, but eventually can't progress past some filters. [[Costi]] figures out if all the shit is accumulating in that room, either someone has to muck it periodically, or there's someway to empty out the floor that he couldn't clearly see. Given how much is there, and some historical staining on the walls, it is possible they will need to clean it soon. [[Costi]] tries to triangulate locations - the door does not line up under the house, there's a decent amount of "other" space that is NW of the store opening, probably in another building nearby. 

[[Jade]] blends her way in, and talks up the sales associate, focusing on ingredient quality and sourcing. This allows [[Molly]] to sneak in, but when he's trying to make it into the back office, triggers an alarm. [[Jade]] covers, and they both leave without issue. We tell [[Molly]] to go back and actually do some petty theft to cover the alarm breaking, he steals an orange with black blobs potion, and thick but clear purple potion. 

On our walk home we go buy both the buildings that [[Costi]] triangulated, one [[Dewstar Alchemicals]] is clearly the connection, the other is an unmarked residential address. 

[[Costi]] looks into the potions we took, they're a Potion of Magma Shield and a Potion of Gravitational Density, [[Rez]] looks into draconic ties - neither expressly has to use dragon scales but both easily could due to the intensity of their inherent magic.
