# Session 205

Before we leave, [[Costi]] goes to the bathroom to scry on [[Baelos]]. Staring into the crystal ball, which fills with raven's feathers before pushed away in a blinding flash of sunlight. He finds an eleven man, blindfolded and shirtless, strung up on chains with an iron collar chaining him to the ceiling. He is suspending entirely by chains. He hasn't been here long, and isn't a warrior. The stone behind him isn't familiar, there are specific joinings of stone resembling sunbursts that are common in temples of [[Pelor]]. It's clear that it is an existing dungeon. 

Meanwhile, [[Freya]] gets some prep and moral boosting on networking with folks who are potentially in the [[Everpresent]].
