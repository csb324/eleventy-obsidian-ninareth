# Session 178

We send [[Samuel]] off to look at the fire in the distance. He shares that it was two humans, a man and woman, [[Thelran Asari]] soldier armor. It's pretty clear he talked to [[Costi]] instead of just cawing. 

[[Malachi]] offers for him and [[Aisha]] to sneak ahead and listen in. They can give a signal if they get in trouble, or have [[Samuel]] tag along and send out a warning. [[Rez]] creeps forward just a touch to be within message distance of them. 

They are surrounded by a small copse of trees, so both [[Malachi]] and [[Aisha]] sneak in just a touch further, and can overhear their conversation. 

They banter for a bit, and mention that they must stay out here and "not in the wagon with everyone else", learn their names are [[Kyle]] and ? . [[Malachi]] begins to notice that things don't quite seem right, the ash is somehow more solid then they are, and their edges almost blur. He tries to get [[Aisha]] and leave, they seem to hear a sound. They see redskinned creatures with iron masks ([[hobgoblin]] [[Iron Shadows]]) violently murder the two guards, then run and vanish around them. As they leave, they see a broken wagon wheel, hundreds of years old. 

[[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] wants to investigate, and [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] is split on if we should. [[Rez]], at [[Malachi]]'s insistence, shares how horrible the memories were once they caught her and warns [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] not to experience it. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] is able to detect that the desecration is concentrated in one spot in the woods, not the center of the fire. 

[[Hugh|Finn]] gives [[Malachi]] a scarf he made, after a brief moment of confusion over his presentation of a paper bag. 

After we reconvene, we agree to try and contain this spot of desecration. [[Costi]] is able to mage hand it into a lead box and put it in [[My Hole]]. It does end the biggest concentration of the desecration, but the fire, and surrounding desecration, does not end. 

[[Freya]] helps lead us again on the path towards the [[Bogwood Outpost]]. She's very fixated, still, on the gruung. [[Malachi]] is shadowing stepping ahead of [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]], scouting along the path. 

We crest a hill, and see [[Bogwood Outpost]] below us. There's an oppressive odor of death: fresh, lingering, and old. [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] can't shake the feeling that we're hearing someone - each of us hear a faint sound of screaming or sobbing, none the same and none clear. There's the structure itself, a guard tower going up about 80 feet and a small garrison beneath it. The entire building has been washed in flame and has holes torn out from it. It's been rebuilt over and over again, and still lies in ruin. The entire thing is sinking into a bog, except a raised bridge to access it. Before that area, there's a few buildings grouped together - 5 or 6 collapsing buildings surround a square filled with stagnant water. 

We slowly begin to make our way into the area. All of the buildings are casting abnormal flickering shadows away from the [[Bogwood Outpost]]. There are two closest to us, on the left, a burned out shell, and on the right and ruined temple to [[Pelor]]. [[Malachi]] traces the outside shadow, and feels pulled to that area. It's dangerous, but complicated - a threat and a plea. He begins moving that direction, and tells us that it's calling to him to investigate first. 

[[Costi]] halfway thinks the statue is following us. [[Rez]] bends to investigate the water, it's not the [[Rotted Grotto]] water, and is much much worse - she sees [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] dead and waterlogged floating in it when she looks too closely. We make our way carefully to the entrance of the [[Pelor]] temple. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] can sense there's another source of desecration inside, and [[Freya]] detect magic and feels an overwhelming amount, and a lot of necromancy. 

Inside, we see a devastated, ruined temple. A blast emanated out from the center, and bones litter the floor. A large alter is at the top of the room, and on it is a scepter - clearly where both the desecration and necromantic magics, alongside conjuration. It's [[Pelor]]ian in design.

[[Malachi]] wraps it in a scarf to lift it, as a ghostly apparition appears. An older man holds the scepter behind the alter, with a book, knife, and goblet laid on the table. He uses the congregation in some dark ritual, slamming the knife into his heart while chanting in abyssal. Stepping out of the ether is a gray creature who rises and takes a bite out of one of the parishioners. 

Around us, a terrible monster, and then several ghasts, appear. [[Rez]] is able to banish it from the first summoning, and after some brief paralyzation, we handle the ghasts without much trouble. 

[[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] investigate the [[Pelor scepter]] again to be sure we don't trigger the same experience. We think we can touch it now, but the desecration, and the plea [[Malachi]] felt, remain. [[Costi]] touch of the [[Raven's Edge]] does yearn to be freed. We debate the need to solve it now, or in the future. 

[[Costi]] and [[Hugh|Finn]] ask their gods to help release the souls here, inspired by [[Rez]], and emboldened by [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]]'s harp music. [[Malachi]] goes to support [[Costi]] driving his sword into the scepter. It glows for a moment, before exploding into light. The scepter is whole, a gleaming symbol of [[Pelor]]. The church is unaffected, but [[Malachi]] feels the anguished pleas are finally quiet.
