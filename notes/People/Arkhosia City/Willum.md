A very handsome face with pronounced cheekbones, a cut jaw, blond hair, with a smirk and pointed ears. He has a scar just under the edge of his left eye. Very charming demeanor. 

Can travel through fire and also other ways? Highly capable lockpick. Talented shot with multiple fire arms.

### Background
The [[Arkhosia (City)]] leader of the [[Fire Raisers]]. A former guard, where he worked with [[Varras]]. 

### Who're They to Us?
Met our party at [[Laughing Hill]] with [[Cut]] and [[Hennurra]]

Instructing [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] on the work needed for the [[Fire Raisers]], was the leader of the cell isn [[Arkhosia (City)]], apparently he was one of the founders. The [[Arkhosia (City)]] and [[Jalantar]] act independently, but [[Willum]] and [[Cinder]] seem to co-lead together.

In the [[Fire Raiser Baddie Audit]], he tried to kill [[The Unforged Few|Our Party]] and was "team-unimaginable-violence" alongside [[Cut]], [[Winifey|Winnie]], and [[Cinder]].

In our fight with [[Cinder]] in the [[Election Day Attack]], he disappeared. Betrayed [[Fire Raisers]] founder [[Victor]]. 

The next time we saw him was with [[Below the Wall]] in [[Thelran Asari]]. Connected us to [[Below the Wall]], has a deal to assassinate [[Chairman Rascryn Lemblehamble]]. Stole from [[Eirian]]'s vaults, but returned it.
