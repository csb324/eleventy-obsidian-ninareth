## Physical Description
Small tiefling, 5ft tall, maroon coloring, gold eyes, dark brown hair braided out of her face. Practical but well made clothing.

## Backstory
Childhood friend to [[Malachi]]. Joined the [[Wayfinders]] after college, but has since resigned -- she didn't feel she was ever very good at it, and didn't like being used to get to [[Malachi]] (who the [[Loombrace Cenacle]] wanted because he's woe-touched).

Graduate from the [[Veraxyes College]], studying artificing and artillery. She is now studying/assisting [[Colvia Ghul]], but hopefully she remains a friend to [[The Unforged Few]].
