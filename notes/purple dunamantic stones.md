# Purple Dunamantic Stones

 they are light and seemingly fragile, but don't seem to break unless the intention is to break. [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]] also notes as she walks past the areas with the purple stones, the air felt weird. A pressure almost. It reminds us of the dunamantic trap that connected us to the plane with the captive dragon. [[Costi]] can tell it's graviturgy and connected to another plane ([[There's Plainly Something Wrong with the Planes]]) - it feels like pinprick in the planes. It's dormant now but it now has a permanent mar in the [[Prime Material]]. We find and circle the spots where all three were used.

Sucked up magical energy through a tiny rip to the [[Plane of Negative Energy]]. They function as a third level counterspell.
