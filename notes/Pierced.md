# Pierced

Ok, I’m going to assume that you literally skip to the end since I told you that it ends up alright. In the last chapter, the woman who fell in love with a dragon is old and on her death bed and her husband is nowhere to be seen. You piece together that he is on some quest to find the golden dragon tooth that’s rumored to be able to turn someone into a dragon if it pierces their heart. He of course makes it back just as she breathes her last breath. He prays to Bahamut. It works out. He turns her into a dragon and they fly off into the sunset. Clearly they worked things out somewhere earlier in the book so just tell me when you come to that realization.
