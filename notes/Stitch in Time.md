# Stitch In Time
Wonderous item (needle), legendary (requires attunement)
Carried by: [[Freya]]

This simple, unassuming silver needle is threaded with a short line of thread.

While attuned, you add +4 to your initiative rolls. Once per day, you can use an action to tie a stitch in your knot. Record your HP and any active status effects on you. For the next 10 minutes, you may use an action to rip that stitch, restoring those effects and HP.

You may also use an action to tear this needle through the fabric of time; in combat, you may use your action to reset initiative, including enemies. After your turn, the new order takes effect.

When used in this way, the stitch ceases to function. Each dawn, roll a d20. On a 18 or higher, it regains all of its magic. On a 2 or below, it permanently loses its magic.
